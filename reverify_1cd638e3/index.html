What this page is. A fresh, independent re-verification of the committed numbers, produced by session arena/1cd638e3-korea3. The verifier reads the committed raw and derived CSV/JSON artefacts directly and does not import the producing pipeline, so a bug in the pipeline cannot certify its own output. Every value is recomputed and linked to the pinned open dataset (nflverse/nfldata@a270278f3a) or the committed file that produces it. The NFL publishes no machine-readable lines, so no line value here is claimed to be an NFL official publication.
Irregularity resolved: F-06 / X02. A prior session flagged that the same-game spread×total φ was “not reproducible from the committed raw file.” The cause is identified: the correlation is computed from the derived data/derived/model_dataset.csv, not from raw data/raw/games.csv. Recomputed from model_dataset.csv, the value reproduces exactly — φ = 0.008609, n = 6784, cover rate 0.488797, χ² = 0.502818. The substantive conclusion (the dependence is far below the φ ≥ 0.0975 a 2-leg −110 parlay needs) is unchanged.

Method

Dataset pin. SHA-256 of every committed data/raw/*.csv is compared to data/provenance.json. The pinned commit was also re-downloaded from codeload.github.com and all five checksums matched byte for byte.
Correlation. The Home-cover × Over φ is recomputed from model_dataset.csv (Pearson correlation of the two binary legs, χ² test of independence) and compared to corr_same_game.csv.
Arithmetic identities. The −110 break-even, the 2-leg −110 parlay decimal and the break-even φ are deterministic identities; no external data is required.
Own-line error. Model and market MAE and the paired-test p-values are recomputed from model_predictions_B.csv and compared to model_metrics.json (variant B).

What is not claimed

No betting price, correlation, ROI or strategy result is invented here, and no funded reward is created. Values re-derived from the open dataset are labelled recomputed; they are not NFL official publications. Blank still means unverified, never zero. Nothing on this page is betting advice.
