Reward rule (pre-registered)

Only strategies passing G1–G9 are reward-eligible. Eligible strategies are ranked by the holdout ROI lower confidence bound, then by Brier skill. Reward points = 100 × (0.6 × normalised holdout lower bound + 0.4 × normalised Brier skill), normalised across eligible strategies only.
