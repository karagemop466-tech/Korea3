No strategy clears all nine validation gates. The best performer — S08 Fade the big favourite — returns 7.60% on 494 bets with a 95% confidence interval of -0.73% … 15.84% and p = 0.023 against a zero-skill simulation. It fails the multiple-testing gate and the season-consistency gate, and its held-out return is ~0. That is the honest state of play, not a validated edge.
