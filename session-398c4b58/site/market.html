Market Structure

Hold, break-even rates, base rates, bias and line movement — the constraints any strategy has to beat.

Before writing a strategy you need to know what the book charges and how often each side wins. All figures below are computed from the same pinned dataset used everywhere else.

Provenance caveat

Median hold of 2.4–2.9% is lower than a typical retail book (≈4.5–5%) and the publisher does not say which sportsbook the lines come from. Treat every ROI figure on this site as conditional on that undocumented source — see IR-01.

If the market were perfectly de-vigged, the actual win rate would match the de-vigged implied rate in every bucket. Favourites priced −200 to −150 underperformed their de-vigged rate by 4.1 points; the longest-priced home dogs beat theirs. This is the classic longshot bias, and it is why the always-favourite control shows a suspicious p-value on the luck page.
