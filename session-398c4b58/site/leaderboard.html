Leaderboard

Scores, tiers, reward points and allocation weights — with the multiple-testing haircut applied.

The competition ranks strategies by a luck-adjusted score rather than raw ROI, because raw ROI rewards small samples and lucky streaks.

score = 100 × (0.40 × gates_passed/9
             + 0.30 × clip((ROI_CI_lower + 5%) / 15%, 0, 1)
             + 0.15 × clip(1 − p_value / 0.10, 0, 1)
             + 0.15 × share_of_seasons_profitable)

Reward tiers: A = passes all nine gates (100 pts), B = score ≥ 55 (60 pts), C = score ≥ 40 (25 pts), D = otherwise (5 pts). Benchmarks earn nothing. Allocation weight is proportional to score² within the ranked pool, so a small score edge translates into a small capital edge, not a linear one.
