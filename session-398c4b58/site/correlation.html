Correlation Values

Every correlation used for parlay and same-game pricing, with confidence intervals and a cross-game control.

These are the numbers that decide whether a combo is priced correctly. All estimates come from settled outcomes in the backtest window and carry a bootstrap confidence interval.

Summary

The headline: spread and total are nearly independent (r ≈ 0.02), while same-player prop legs are heavily dependent (r up to 0.92). Correlation edge lives in player markets, not in the main markets.
