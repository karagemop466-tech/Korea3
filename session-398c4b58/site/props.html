Player Props

Prop origination, calibration diagnostics and prop-combo strategies — with the price limitation stated up front.

Read before using these numbers

No verifiable player-prop prices exist in this environment. Every prop strategy is settled against an assumed −110 price and a synthetic line (the player's trailing 8-game median). Hit rates below are measured and real; ROI is conditional on both assumptions. These strategies are excluded from the leaderboard ranking — see IR-03.

How a prop market is defined here

  • Naive line — median of the player's last 8 games, rounded to the nearest half point.
  • Our number — median of the player's last 4 games (a faster-reacting estimate of the same functional, so the comparison is not contaminated by the mean/median gap).
  • Probability — empirical map estimated on prior seasons only: the realised over-rate inside each bin of (our number − line)/σ, shrunk toward 50/50 and forced monotone.
  • Pushes — refunded, never counted as losses.

Honest reading

Our prop probabilities are calibrated (predicted vs realised track closely, see the table below) but they carry almost no skill: a Brier score of 0.2439–0.2484 against 0.25 for a coin flip is a 1–2% improvement. Any large ROI below therefore measures how stale the synthetic line is, not forecasting ability.
