Origination order: side, then total, then moneyline

The pipeline order the desk originates markets in, the sign conventions it uses, and the checks that enforce it.

The order: side → total → moneyline

For every game this repository originates lines for, the side (spread) is originated first, the total second, and the moneyline third. The order is not cosmetic: the side and the total are the two quantities the model forecasts directly, and the moneyline is derived from the side forecast — never the other way round. A moneyline opinion must never leak into the side or the total.
