Leaderboard — signal separated from staking

Reward eligibility is decided on the signal axis (flat-unit ROI, edge, hit rate); staking is reported separately and can never promote a strategy.

Two axes, deliberately separated

A staking plan can turn a negative-edge signal into a positive ledger (and a lucky staking run can hide a bad signal). This leaderboard therefore reports signal and staking as two independent axes with different columns and different gates:
  • Signal axis — what the strategy knows: bets, hit rate, mean edge, and ROI at a flat 1 unit per bet. Reward eligibility is decided on this axis only.
  • Staking axis — what a bankroll plan would have done on the same bets, as a documented variant: fractional Kelly capped at 2% of bankroll per bet. It is reported for transparency and can never promote a strategy.
