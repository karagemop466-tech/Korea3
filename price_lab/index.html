Purpose

This desk is the settlement layer for the repository's line-origination research: it stores what the market said (open and close) about every market we originate, settles outcomes against officially cross-checked results, and scores strategies on a leaderboard designed so that luck in staking cannot mask a bad signal. A demotion gate is defined before anything goes live. Nothing on these pages is a betting recommendation.
