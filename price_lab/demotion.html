Demotion gate: sending a live strategy back to paper

Six automatic triggers, measured on the live ledger, defined before anything goes live.

The demotion gate

Promotion gates send a paper strategy live; the demotion gate is the way back. It is defined before anything is live, so that demotion is mechanical rather than negotiable. Status: proposed spec — nothing is live, so nothing is demoted — the owner still has to adopt it alongside the promotion gates on the competition page.
