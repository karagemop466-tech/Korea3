The honest state of the snapshot store. A closing number exists for every game in the store, and a price for most of them. An opening number exists for 272 games in one season from one book, and that file has no price column. So the store can answer "what did the market close at" and cannot answer "what could I have bet at kickoff-minus-a-week". Closing-line value needs the second, which is why it is not computed anywhere on this site.
