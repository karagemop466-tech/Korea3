Promotion & demotion

Eight gates, applied fail-closed: a strategy clears all of them to stay live, and a single failure returns it to paper with the reason on the row.

The rule is simple and it is enforced in code. A strategy is live only while it clears every gate. Any single failure sends it back to paper: still tracked, still measured, no longer staked. The gate is fail-closed — a missing or unreadable input counts as a failure, because "we cannot tell" is not evidence of an edge. That is why nothing on this site is live: the inputs a strategy needs to stay live do not exist here.

Competition and rewards

The brief asks for a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. The board exists and the gates are enforced. The reward does not, and is not described. No sponsor, no amount, no terms and no entry are stated, because none exist. A number invented here would be a commitment nobody has made.

If a prize is introduced, the gates above are the entry condition, the leaderboard is the ranking, and the questions register lists what must be settled first — the licence, the price archive, and what happens when a live strategy is demoted. Those are Q01, Q04 and Q09.
