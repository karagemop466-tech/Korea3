Depth, not the fact of a replacement — and only what this build read. The brief asks whether a club is down to a 2nd, 3rd or 4th string. Every row below was transcribed by this build from the club's own published chart and joined to the official NFL injury report for Week 10 of 2026. This section does not republish another session's capture: it publishes only the 5 rows it verified itself, across 8 clubs, because a row inherited from an unverified capture would be an unverified claim. The small number is the honest number.

The join rule, in full

A row exists when the first name a club prints at a position also appears on the NFL's official Week 10 2026 injury report for that club with a Game Status of Out or Doubtful. The replacement is the second name the same club prints at the same position. Neither column is an official designation of who will start.

Why five rows and not forty

The alternative was to copy another build's capture. This page explains why that was refused.

An earlier build of this repository published 43 starter replacements across 18 clubs. Those rows were captured by a previous session, and this session did not read the pages behind them. Republishing them here would put numbers on this site that this build cannot defend, which is the exact failure mode the whole project is built to avoid.

So the page publishes the five rows this build transcribed and joined itself, and names the gap: 8 of 32 clubs read, 3 with a matched replacement. Widening the capture is proposal SG06 on the suggestions page, and the staleness problem it would need to solve is question Q16 on the questions page.
