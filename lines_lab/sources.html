What "official" is allowed to mean here. The NFL publishes rules, scoring, schedules, injury reports and settlement mechanics; licensed operators publish their own house rules; US standards bodies publish statistical methods. None of them publishes an NFL point spread, total, price or correlation. So an official source can establish how a bet settles and cannot establish what the market was. Every market number on this site is labelled dataset, and the tier is printed on the row.

What the dataset is not

Released by the nflverse project. An independent open-source distribution of data collected from public sources — not an NFL publication.

It is not an NFL publication, so no value from it is labelled official anywhere on this site. The NFL's own terms prohibit systematic retrieval of its services without written consent (NFL-TERMS ↗), which is why this section uses a pinned open dataset rather than scraping the league or a book.

Every table row links its source. A blank cell is an unverified value and links to Flagged for review. Market numbers come from the pinned nflverse dataset at commit a270278f3ac4; the NFL does not publish betting prices, so no market value here is labelled official.
