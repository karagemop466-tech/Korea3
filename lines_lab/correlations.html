The rule on this page: no n, no number. Every coefficient below is computed from the pinned dataset at build time and printed with the number of settled games it rests on and the tier of its input. A coefficient that cannot reach n = 30 is reported as not computed rather than printed. And none of these is an official number: no league or operator publishes a betting correlation, so labelling one "official" would be false. They are dataset values, and they say so.
