How the analysis was done

The method is stated so a reader can attack it rather than trust it.

Eight decisions that determine whether the numbers mean anything.
MethodHow it was doneAuthorityWhere it is shown
Sign conventionPositive spread_line = the HOME team is favoured. Settled against an official box score, not a column heading: DAL 29 @ TB 31 is a home margin of +2 and the dataset stores it as +10.0.NFL-PS ↗Findings & analysis
SettlementA bet is settled from the final score, in code: the side covers when margin > line, the total goes over when the sum > total_line, a tie pushes. The dataset's own outcome column is then compared, not trusted.NFL-PS ↗Verified results
Leakage controlTeam ratings are updated only after a game is settled; rolling form uses shift(1). No feature of a game is visible before kickoff.code ↗Signal tests
Walk-forwardThe fair line is refitted on every prior season and tested on the next. No random splits, no shuffling of games, no future information.NIST-CI ↗Fair line vs market
Multiplicity18 rules are tested once each. The threshold is 0.05/18, the Bonferroni bound, so the chance of any false pass across the family is held at 5%.NIST-BONF ↗Signal tests
Sample sizeThe number of settled bets needed to distinguish an edge from zero is computed with the NIST one-sided formula at 80% power, not chosen by feel.NIST-SAMPLESIZE ↗Signal tests
BlanksA value with no source is blank. It is never zero, never the median, never an average of its neighbours. Blanks are counted and published.code ↗Flagged for review
DemotionThe gate is fail-closed: a missing input demotes the strategy. This is why nothing is live — the inputs that would keep a strategy live do not exist here.NIST-CI ↗Promotion & demotion
