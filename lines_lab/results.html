Why a results feed has to be verified before it is used. A backtest is only as good as its settlement. This page re-derives the outcome of every market from the final score in code, then compares that derivation with the outcome column the dataset publishes. Where they disagree, neither is silently preferred: the row is listed below with both values, and where the disagreement mattered a third source was opened and is linked.

Settlement rules, and where each comes from

The rule is fixed from an official source before any number is computed.

Five settlement rules, each fixed before it is applied.
MarketRule applied in codeAuthorityThe sentence it rests on
SpreadA side covers when its margin exceeds the line. If the margin equals the line the bet pushes and the stake is returned.NFL-PS ↗«if the point spread was +10/-10 ... you would push and get your initial wager returned»
TotalThe over wins when the two scores sum above the number, the under when below, a push when equal. Same push rule as the spread.NFL-PS ↗«the odds for point spread are -110» — the same reference price applies to a total
MoneylineThe side that wins outright pays its price. A tied game pushes; the price does not settle.NFL-ML ↗«Tie | Push | If the game ends in a tie, the bettor's wager is returned»
Reference priceWhere no price exists the row is marked line-only and no ROI is computed. A -110 default is never substituted.NFL-PS ↗«Most often, the betting odds for point spread are -110»
ParlayEvery leg must win. A pushed leg is removed and the price recalculated.DK-PARLAY ↗operator house rule on parlay settlement
