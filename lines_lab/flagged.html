What was done with a flag

Nothing is repaired. The pipeline does not rewrite the input data, does not drop a disagreeing row and does not fill a blank. A flag changes what the site claims, never what the data says.

One exception is recorded, and it is this session's own error. Item U05 is a mistake in this pipeline, not in the data: the first reading of the sign convention was inverted. It was caught, corrected against an official box score, and every dependent number was recomputed. It is listed here rather than quietly fixed, because a reader who found it independently would otherwise have no way to tell whether other numbers were also wrong. They were re-run.
