Executive summary

The result in one page: what was built, what it shows, what cannot be decided, and the one input that would change the answer.

Bottom line. This section does what the brief asked and reaches a negative result. The price store, the results feed, the fair-vs-market columns, the signal/staking split, the depth-of-replacement join, the correlations-with-n and the demotion gate are all built and verified. What they show is that this dataset does not contain a betting edge that survives validation. The value of the section is the machinery that says so, and the specific list of information that would be needed to try again honestly.

The single change that would most improve this

Buy or build a timestamped, two-sided price archive. Every blocked item on this site — closing-line value, live-vs-paper comparison, a promotion that is not curve-fitting, a defensible competition — depends on knowing what price was available at a known moment. One book's opening line for 272 games, with no price, is not that archive.

It is the top item on the suggestions page, the blocking input on the questions page, and the reason the demotion gate can only demote.
