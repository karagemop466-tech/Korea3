The three columns the brief asks for, and why the difference is not an edge. Every row publishes the model's fair line, the market's line and the difference as separate columns, never merged into one "pick". A difference is not a bet: it is a disagreement, and on this data the market wins the disagreement in every season. A model that disagrees with a better forecast is not finding value; it is making an error the market has already priced.

Why the origination order is side → total → moneyline

The order is enforced in code, not just described.

Four markets and the order in which each is originated.
MarketOrderWhy it sits thereAuthorityCoverage
1. SideFIRSTThe only market with a sign convention the NFL defines, the longest history (1999–2026) and the tightest prices. If the side cannot be originated, nothing downstream is worth originating.NFL-PS ↗1999–2026
2. TotalSECONDNeeds the same inputs but has a weaker reference: a total push rate of 99 games in 7,075 is 1.4%, and the market for totals is less efficient in a way this data can see but not exploit.NFL-PS ↗1999–2026
3. MoneylineTHIRDA non-linear transform of the side. Originating it first would launder a side error into a price. It is only originated once the side exists, and it inherits the side's error.NFL-ML ↗2006–2026 priced
Player propsNOT ORIGINATEDNo player-level market exists in the dataset. A fair prop line here would be invented, so none is produced.DK-PROP ↗—
