A parlay is arithmetic, not a signal. Everything on this page is the arithmetic of combining legs at a stated price with a stated correlation. It is not a prediction and not a recommendation. Where a posted price would be needed — a real same-game parlay, a prop combo — the value is blank, because no posted price exists in this data and inventing one would be the error this project exists to avoid.

Fair value of a 2-leg parlay, by leg probability and correlation

P(AB) = P(A)P(B) + φ·√(P(A)(1−P(A))P(B)(1−P(B))), priced at -110 per leg (3.6446 decimal).

A negative φ makes a parlay worse than the independent calculation; a positive φ makes it better. At φ = 0 the parlay is the product, and the whole cost is the vig.

Player-prop combos: why nothing is priced

The brief asks for player-prop bet combos. To price one you need, per leg, a player's market line, that market's price, and the joint correlation with the other legs. This repository holds none of the three: the dataset is one row per game, with no player statistics, no prop lines and no prop prices. The existing props page records the same gap from a different build.

What is published instead is the arithmetic above, which is correct for any two legs once a reader supplies their own leg probabilities and correlation. That is a tool, not a forecast, and the page labels it as a tool.
