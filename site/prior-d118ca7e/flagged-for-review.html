F-01 — nfl.com standings pages ship unrendered template text

Source rendering

The official standings HTML contains literal placeholders ($year$ NFL $displayname$ Standings), a 'your browser is not supported' banner and team names decorated with tie-break glyphs (xz*, xy). Only the numeric columns W/L/T/PF/PA were transcribed; badges, streaks and seeding were deliberately ignored so no value could be misread.

Source for manual review: https://www.nfl.com/standings/division/2025/REG

Handling: No fix required. The transcription rule is documented in data/official/verification_attestation.json; the transcribed values themselves were removed from the repository under F-10.

resolved

F-02 — 17-game season with ties (DAL 7-9-1, GB 9-7-1) — sums check out

Standings arithmetic

Both 2025 records include one tie and 17 games. Derived team-slots (W+L+T over all 32 teams) = 544 = 2 x 272 regular-season games, and total PF = PA = 12,519. No anomaly.

Source for manual review: https://www.nfl.com/standings/division/2025/REG

Handling: Closed.

F-03 — Closing odds fields are uneven across seasons

Market data coverage

Moneylines are blank before 2006 and some spread/total prices are missing. Every strategy is priced at the price carried in the dataset and falls back to the standard -110 only where the dataset has no price; no odds were imputed from outside sources. Moneyline strategies therefore start in 2006 and report a smaller denominator.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata

Handling: Reported in the field-coverage table on Data &amp; verification.

F-04 — Player-week statistics could not be obtained from a verifiable source

Player props

Player-week statistics could not be obtained from a verifiable source inside this build environment. Evidence: nflverse release assets (player_stats_*, stats_player_week_*) live on release-assets.githubusercontent.com, which is blocked from the build sandbox (TLS failure recorded in COORDINATION.md). Every reachable GitHub mirror of stats_player_week_*.csv was inspected and is a truncated or synthetic test fixture: Eric-Weng/CommishDesk contains team codes XXX/YYY and a fabricated player; Fibonacci-0112/NFL-360 is cut at 76 rows; balprab24/fantasy-kai ships a single-season fixture only. Consequence: prop lines, prop baselines and prop correlations are NOT published as numbers.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases

Handling: Player props page publishes the framework, gates and required sample sizes only.

F-05 — Opening lines exist for one season only, and are not a same-week open

CLV

data/initial_lines.csv covers the 2021 season from a single book (WSGT). The mean absolute gap between that snapshot and the reference closing line is 9.653 points, far larger than a normal open-to-close move, so it behaves like a lookahead/early snapshot. Closing-line value therefore cannot be established from verified data here and gate G6 is recorded as informational (n/a), not as a pass.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata

Handling: Gate G6 shows n/a for every strategy; no strategy earns credit for CLV.

F-06 — Weather coverage is incomplete and contains implausible outliers

Weather fields

Wind/temperature are populated for 5,251 of 7,384 played games (71%) and almost never for dome games (2 of 1,848), so all weather tests are outdoor-only by construction. Coverage collapses in 2022 (38%) and 2026 (42%). Outliers such as 70 mph (2008_02_TEN_CIN) and 71 mph (2016_13_NYG_PIT) look like source errors and were left untouched.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata

Handling: Weather signals are reported on the covered subsample with the caveat attached; no value was corrected.

F-07 — No official team-total or player-prop line exists in verified data

Team totals / SGP pricing

The correlation engine measures leg dependence from official final scores, and prices parlays against a hypothetical book that multiplies -110 legs independently. Real sportsbooks apply correlation-aware pricing to same-game parlays, so the large 'EV' figures for highly correlated legs (e.g. home ML + home cover) are a measurement of independent-pricing error, NOT an available bet. Team-total legs use half the closing game total as a proxy and are labelled as such.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata

Handling: Labelled on the Parlays page; no parlay edge is claimed.

F-08 — Own spread model is less accurate than the market and drifts in the holdout

Own-line model

Mean absolute error versus the actual margin: 10.67 (model) vs 10.435 (market) in the design window, and 10.174 vs 9.635 in the holdout. The model also under-predicts the home margin by 0.919 points in the holdout (bias is measured as model minus actual, so a positive value means the model predicts MORE home margin than actually occurs). This is reported as a limitation, not corrected after the fact.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata

Handling: Own-lines page reports accuracy honestly; no post-hoc recalibration on holdout data.

F-09 — 2026 season is incomplete at build time

Season in progress

7384 played games span 1999-2026; the 2026 regular season is captured through the games played on the build date, and unplayed 2026 games carry no score. All 32 teams' 2026 W/L/T/PF/PA still matched nfl.com exactly at build time.

Source for manual review: https://www.nfl.com/standings/division/2026/REG

Handling: Re-run pipeline/verify.py after any new week to refresh.

F-10 — NFL.com terms restrict reproduction and systematic retrieval — transcriptions removed

Data rights

NFL.com terms sections 1.1 and 1.3 restrict reproduction of its content and require express prior written consent for systematic retrieval that creates or compiles a collection or database; the official NFL gamebook carries a similar use notice. This project holds no NFL licence. Action taken: the 64 rows of NFL-sourced standings values that were transcribed to run the verification check were deleted from the repository. What remains is data/official/verification_attestation.json (counts, team codes, URLs, date) and the derived values computed from the open dataset. Nothing NFL-sourced is redistributed; no scraping is automated anywhere in the pipeline.

Source for manual review: https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms/

Handling: Complied. The live comparison re-runs only if a rights holder supplies a CSV with the documented schema; the site marks the check as attested rather than re-runnable until then.

F-11 — The official check is a team-aggregate reconciliation, not per-game verification

Verification scope

W/L/T/PF/PA for each team is reconciled against the official standings, which constrains the outcome of every game in the season but does not read each game's box score against an official source. No per-game official verification was performed in this build and none is claimed. The spread/total/odds fields, which have no official public source at all, remain third-party snapshots (F-03, F-07).

Source for manual review: https://www.nfl.com/standings/division/2025/REG

Handling: Wording corrected across the site, the executive summary and the README.

F-12 — Every ROI figure is a backtest on a third-party dataset, not a settlement record

Performance claims

Costs, limits, line availability, void rules and the actual price a bettor could have obtained are not evidenced here. Results should be read as a research backtest on historical closing-line snapshots. No wager, price, execution or payout is claimed, and no strategy is claimed to be profitable.

Source for manual review: https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata

Handling: Stated on the site, in the executive summary and in the README.
