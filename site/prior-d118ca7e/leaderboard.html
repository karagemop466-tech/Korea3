Reward mechanics. A strategy earns a share of the 1,000-unit pool proportional to its score, but only if it passes every applicable validation gate (G1–G7, see Validation gates). Score = 1.0 × full-sample ROI(%) + 1.5 × holdout ROI(%) + 2.0 × t-statistic − 0.15 × max drawdown(units). The purpose of the pool is to make "no allocation" a real outcome: it is the outcome today, and it is the correct one.
