Open tasks for a follow-up session

  1. B01 — prop lines. Connect a licensed odds feed, or collect posted prop prices manually and store them with timestamps. Until then, no prop correlation can be measured.
  2. B02 — injuries. Machine-readable inactives would be the single biggest modelling improvement.
  3. B03 — weather provenance. Join station records to venue and kickoff time; then S14 can be judged on verified inputs rather than unverified ones.
  4. B04 / B05 — referees and travel. Both need external, verified sources before a signal is worth testing.
  5. F07 — prices. A complete price history would remove the largest compromise on the site (26.9% imputation).
  6. F09 — operator rules. Confirm the DraftKings house-rules copy against the live page.
