Staking rules the board enforces

  • Flat 1 unit. Every published record is flat-staked. Kelly sizing on an unvalidated edge is leverage on noise.
  • Price discipline. A rule is only credited at the price recorded in the dataset. If your book offers worse, the record does not apply.
  • No parlays inside a single-bet record. Combining legs is a separate page with a separate gate.
  • Push handling. Returned stakes are excluded from both the numerator and the denominator.
  • One bet per game per rule. No re-betting a moved line, no middling the same game twice.

What would actually earn a promotion

A rule needs all eight gates. The binding constraint for every provisional rule is G4, and G4 is a function of two things: the size of the edge and the number of rules searched. There are only two honest ways to pass it — collect more bets at the same edge, or stop searching (a smaller family means a lower multiplicity penalty). The second is the uncomfortable one: the more strategies you test, the higher the bar each of them must clear. Any leaderboard that ignores this rewards production, not skill.
