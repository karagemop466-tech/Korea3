Correlation lab

Correlation is the only quantity that can turn a losing parlay into a winning one, so it is measured here rather than asserted. Every value on this page is computed from 7,075 games with confidence intervals.

Pairs in the same market are structurally correlated — if rule A backs the home side of a market and rule B backs the away side on the same game, their outcomes are complementary by construction. Only the different-market pairs carry information, and their median |φ| is 0.046.

Why this page matters more than the signal pages

A single bet at −110 has a 52.38% break-even. A parlay's break-even depends on a quantity — the joint probability — that the bettor cannot see directly and the book can price however it likes. Measuring φ is the only way to know whether a parlay is priced fairly. Given that the measured φ for the most popular combination is +0.0086, against a required 0.0975, the answer for that combination is no.
