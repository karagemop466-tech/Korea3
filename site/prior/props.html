Player props

Blocked by data availability, not by method. This page lists the exact inputs required, the estimator that will run when they exist, and nothing else.

Nothing on this page is estimated. Player-prop markets require two inputs that this research environment cannot reach: an official player-level game log and licensed prop lines with prices. Every prop value on the site is blank and listed on Flagged for review.

Estimator (written, waiting for inputs)

pipeline/props.py ↗

  1. phi between binary leg outcomes at matched thresholds (e.g. QB passing yards 275.5 and WR receiving yards 65.5)
  2. joint hit rate vs product of marginals for every same-game pair, with week-block bootstrap CI
  3. threshold-swept conditional probability P(leg B over | leg A over) to expose non-linear dependence
  4. delta-method or bootstrap CI on the correlation, plus n and missingness per pair

What player props would add if the inputs existed

Player props are the market where public models most often claim an edge, because a book must price hundreds of correlated thresholds from one game. The measurement plan is the same as the one already working for game markets: walk-forward pricing, week-block bootstrap intervals, a de-vigged market benchmark, and a luck band. Without the two inputs above, any number published here would be a fabrication, so none is published.
