Research

Signal library

18 rules, all pre-registered, all reported. Backtested on 7,075 priced regular-season games from 2000 to 2026, scored against the closing line at the recorded price.

Protocol

  • Universe: every completed regular-season game with a closing spread and total — 6,827 games after requiring model coverage.
  • Price: the price actually recorded for that side where the dataset has one, otherwise the documented standard −110. The imputed share is published per rule on Single Bets.
  • Pushes: stake returned, excluded from win-rate and ROI denominators, counted separately.
  • Windows: DISCOVERY 2000–2015, CONFIRMATION 2016–2026. A rule must be positive in both.
  • No deletions: rules that lost money are published in full. That is the point of a leaderboard competition — you cannot rank skill if the losers are hidden.
