Parlay arithmetic with our own measured correlation, plus the two parlay strategies that were actually settled.

Break-even dependence for a two-leg −110 parlay is φ = 0.09751: below it the operator holds a margin, above it the bettor would be getting a fair or better price. The largest same-game dependence measured here is far above it, which is why same-game legs must never be priced as independent.
