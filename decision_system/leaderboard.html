Why the ranking is not the ROI column

Eleven zero-edge strategies with the same bet counts produce a best ROI of +2.4% on average and a profitable winner in 100.0% of simulations. Ranking raw ROI therefore rewards the luckiest of eleven tries. The leaderboard instead requires a deflated Sharpe probability of at least 0.95 (multiple-comparison reference), a season-block bootstrap, and an untouched holdout.

Reward rule (proposed, not funded)

  • Enter only with a pre-registration commit created before the first backtest.
  • Reach at least 1,000 settled selections (5,000 for a reward claim).
  • Pass all seven gates on the backtest and hold the sign over a forward paper-trading period of at least 200 settled selections.
  • Rewards, if any are ever funded, are paid on the forward record only.
