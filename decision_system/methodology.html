Known limitations

  • One price per game; no book range, so every ROI is conditional.
  • Player-level measurement is limited to the seasons with a pinned snap file.
  • Injury data is a transcription and is not reconciled with the official pages.
  • No in-game injury data.
  • No licensed prop prices.
  • The repository files were produced by different sessions over two days; this build reads them read-only and never regenerates them.
