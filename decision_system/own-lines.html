Originating our own line

How a number is produced before the market posts one, and the only test that matters: does our number beat the closing line more often than a coin flip.

The honest result. The repository's own-line model is measurably worse than the closing line on mean absolute error. That is the finding, not a failure of the build: a reproducible process that loses to the market is the baseline every claimed edge has to beat.

The line-origination numbers themselves live in the root dashboard (live lines, strategies) and the site build; this page is the decision-layer summary so that one number is not published twice from two different pipelines.
