Depth & backups

Depth, starters and backups

First string, second, third and fourth — derived from how many snaps each player actually took, because no official numbered depth chart was found.

No official depth chart exists on NFL.com. The club depth-chart route returned a 404 page when opened on 2026-11-12. Every 'string' label here is therefore derived from snap rank within the position group, and is labelled unofficial: DS-F01.

Offence and defence are tracked separately

A backup quarterback changes the offence; a backup corner changes the defence. The engine builds two separate indices and never pools them into a single "injuries" score, because the market does not treat them the same way.
