How much two legs of a combination move together, measured on 7,075 priced regular-season games. Every value is ours; no operator publishes one.

What this means for a parlay. Only the moneyline × spread pair is strongly dependent (φ ≈ 0.79). Spread × total is indistinguishable from zero, and legs from two different games in the same week are independent in this data. A combination of independent legs is priced fairly by multiplying the legs; a dependent pair is not, and the operator's own rules allow it to restrict such parlays.
