How blanks are handled

A blank cell on this site always links back to this page. It means "the data needed to compute this does not exist in a reachable, verifiable source". It never means zero, and no value is ever interpolated, back-filled or estimated to fill the space. The site's build check fails if a blank is rendered without the flag link.
