This verifier can prove three things: that the pages match their data, that the data's arithmetic matches the artifacts it cites, and that the artifacts still hash to the pinned commit. It cannot prove that the pinned third-party prices were real, that an official page still says what it said at capture time, or that any strategy has skill. The decision rules prove that no choice has been recorded; they cannot prove that the right choice was made. Nothing here is official, because the league publishes no lines, prices or returns - which is the finding, not an omission.
