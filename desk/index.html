What this desk is

  • A generated surface: every page in desk/ is emitted by desk/build.py from desk/data/*.json. Edit the JSON, never the HTML.
  • An availability ledger: every designation row is transcribed from an official NFL.com publication captured 2026-11-13 and links to that publication on the row.
  • A reconciliation, not a restatement: where two official publishers disagree for the same game, both readings are printed side by side.
  • A pre-decision register: suggestions carry options and no decision; the owner chooses, then anything is built.

And what it is not

  • Not betting advice. Nothing here has cleared the promotion gates in desk/gates.html.
  • Not a depth chart. No official publisher in this cycle provided 1st/2nd/3rd/4th-string ordering; every string-order cell is blank and flagged.
  • Not a live feed. The capture is a point-in-time read of pages that clubs update through the week.
  • Not a market feed. The prices behind the signal numbers come from one pinned third-party dataset, labelled as such on every row; no market figure here carries an official status.

Transcription policy. Designations and headlines are transcribed by hand from the fetched page text into desk/data/*.json, with the player URL exactly as the publisher printed it. desk/check.py validates structure (status vocabulary, unique club/player pairs, source host, row counts, coverage of all 32 clubs) but cannot re-fetch the page, so a reader verifying a row should open the link on that row.
