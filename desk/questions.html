KQ02 · What does a blank Game Status mean: 'not yet published', 'not applicable', or 'certain to play'? · Injury data answered this cycle blocking

Known. The official policy wording retrieved this cycle states that a player certain to play is no longer listed in the club's Game Status Report, that Probable was eliminated, and that a such a player who is then deactivated requires an explanation to the league. Applied to the captured data, this is the only reading that makes the LAC and BAL rows coherent (17 and 8 rows, all with a blank status, on the eve of a Monday game).

Not known. Whether that wording is the rule in force in 2026: the retrieved page carries no date and no 'in force from' statement (flag F02).

How to find out. Find a dated policy document (Game Operations Manual, bylaws amendment, or a league memo) or a newer Competition Committee article. Until then, treat 'certain to play' as a labelled interpretation, not as a fact.

Who can answer. NFL league operations; the owner decides the evidence bar (K03).

Answer. The blank is meaningful, not missing: under the published revision, a player certain to play is removed from the Game Status Report, so an empty cell alongside a DNP or limited practice entry is an availability statement. This desk publishes the interpretation with its caveat and does not impute a designation. settled by
