Decision memo - 2026 week 10, captured 2026-11-13T23:04:21Z

Five paragraphs, then the numbers.

1. The site now answers the question it was built for, and the answer is a gap

The brief asks the system to know which backups replace starters. This cycle the official pages support the first half of that sentence for all 32 clubs and the second half for two of them. The league publishes who is out and, occasionally, a coach sentence naming a replacement; it publishes no depth ordering anywhere an agent can verify. That single fact decides three of the fourteen open suggestions and is the reason no replacement-quality signal can be built from official data this season.

2. What was newly established

  • The four clubs missing from last cycle's capture are on bye - named by the official scores page, not inferred (F01, resolved).
  • A blank game-status cell is not missing data: under the league's published revision, a player certain to play is removed from the report. That changes how every injury feature must be coded (Q02 answered; the rule's currency is still open, F02).
  • One designation is published under three different player URLs by three official pages, so the join key the whole pipeline needs does not exist yet (F08/Q03 evidence).
  • Two of the three official pages for the same week contradict each other on a live case: a Texan listed questionable and ruled out on the same Friday (N01).

3. The state of the line-origination project

Recomputed from the artifacts rather than restated: 11 strategies in one ledger, 18 signals in another, 15 ranked rows in a third, 8 rows a fourth refuses to score. None is eligible anywhere. The originated line is competitive on error but not on outcome correlation, and the strongest raw result in the repository (+8.41% over 607 bets) becomes +2.48% after the shrinkage the project's own gate applies - and its q across a family of 18 is 0.288. The competition therefore has no winner, which is the correct output of honest gates and the reason the reward design matters more than any single rule.

4. What is blocked, and on what

15 questions are open and 13 of them block a decision. Every blocking question has the same shape: the missing thing is not an analysis, it is an input (ordered depth, a price source, a roster-status feed, a capture cadence) or an owner decision (site authority, reward form, evidence bar for rules). {s['decision_gate']['counts']['Proposed - awaiting owner decision']} suggestions are on the board with options and costs attached; the build refuses to let any of them be recorded as approved here.

5. What I would do first, if a path were chosen today

K02 and K07 cost almost nothing and remove two classes of silent error (bye-week false alarms, as-of-date contradictions). K11 turns capture into an event log, which is the only change that makes the injury signal testable at all. K06 has to land before any reward exists, because 15 strategy IDs currently name two different rules and a leaderboard that pays by ID can pay the wrong entry. K01 is not research and is the cheapest quality win available: pick a front door, and this desk becomes a subpage of it instead of a tenth tree.
