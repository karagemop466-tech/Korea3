The promotion policy

Twelve gates, consolidated from the three systems this repository already runs. A strategy is ranked, rewarded or quoted to anyone only when every non-blocking row below passes and the blocking rows have an owner decision attached to them.

The twelve policy rows above are a consolidation, not a new claim of skill: they are what the repository's own three validation systems demand, expressed once so a strategy can be judged the same way in all three.

Two gate vocabularies already exist and they collide: scripts/gates.py defines G1..G8 with one meaning (sample size, ROI, both-window, multiplicity, concentration, price stress, second source, shrinkage) while line_lab/evaluation.py defines G1..G9 with different meanings (priced share, causality, ... holdout ROI, CI+FDR, Brier skill, stability, priced share, permutation). A row that says 'fails G4' means two different things depending on which ledger produced it. That is flag F08, and it is why the rows here are keyed P01..P12 rather than G1..G9.

Nothing on this page is an official NFL figure. No gate can be closed with league-published data alone: prices, settlement and lines come from the pinned third-party dataset, which is why the blocking rows say so.
