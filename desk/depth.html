Depth and vacancies

A vacancy is an official designation. A fill exists only where a publisher named one.

captured 2026-11-13T23:04:21Z5 open high-severity flags15 blocking-or-open questions0 strategies eligible for a reward14 suggestions awaiting your decisionread the flags first

The requirement, stated plainly

The brief asks the system to know which backups replace starters — second, third, fourth string — on both sides of the ball. Below is every vacancy the official pages support this week, and what each one could and could not say about the fill.

The brief asks for second, third and fourth-string backups replacing starters. Officially, only the first half of that sentence exists this cycle: who is unavailable (the weekly report) and, occasionally, who replaces them (a coach statement inside a news roundup).

No official publisher in this cycle provides depth ordering, so every depth-string cell in this directory is blank and flagged. That is a data gap, not an oversight, and it is the largest missing input for the stated goal.

Two paths close it, and both are owner decisions rather than agent decisions: admit a licensed or club-published ordered depth source with its own verification cadence (suggestion K04), or define a proxy from official data - the highest-listed player at that position who is not OUT or DOUBTFUL - and label it a proxy everywhere it appears (suggestion K05).

A proxy is computable today from A01 plus position groups, needs no new licence, and fails loudly when a club's whole position group is OUT. It is not a depth chart and must never be published as one.
