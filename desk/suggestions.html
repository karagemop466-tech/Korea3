How to record a decision

  1. Record the choice in one command: python3 desk/decide.py <K-id> <option letter> --date YYYY-MM-DD --note "why". It writes both files, rebuilds the pages and runs the verifier.
  2. Or edit by hand: in desk/data/suggestions.json set decision_status (Approved / Rejected / Deferred), chosen_option to the exact option text and decision_date; then append one entry to desk/data/decisions.json using the shape printed on the page.
  3. Run python3 desk/build.py && python3 desk/check.py. The status strip, the impact tables, the flag register and the question register are all recomputed from those two files, so nothing has to be edited twice.
  4. check.py refuses: a state with no record, a record with no state, an option text that is not one of the suggestion's options, a date that disagrees between the two files, an approved suggestion whose flags stay open, and 'Implemented' from inside the desk.

What moves, and what does not

  • Moves: the Decision impact table on index.html and suggestions.html; the flag register: a flag closed by an adopted suggestion moves out of the open count and is reported as retired by decision, not by discovery; the question register: a question unblocked by a decision is re-listed as answered by the owner, with the decision entry as its source; the blocking counts in the status strip on every page
  • Does not move: the captured rows: no decision changes what an official page printed on the capture date; the arithmetic on the signals and gates pages: those numbers are recomputed from the repository's artifacts and stay as they are until an artifact changes; any other site tree: desk/ writes only inside desk/, so a suggestion whose option touches root navigation is a request, not an edit

Before a decision is recorded, every impact field prints as 'nothing moves yet' rather than as a forecast of what the desk would do. That is deliberate: a proposed suggestion is not a plan of work, and this session's brief says the choice is the owner's.
