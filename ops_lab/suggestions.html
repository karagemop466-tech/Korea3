How to choose

the decision rule for the reviewer

  • Pick by unblocking power per risk: SG-ops1 and SG-ops5 buy real open/close evidence for every future market; SG-ops3 removes a known sign-flipping hazard; SG-ops7 makes the safety net automatic.
  • Reject anything whose evidence is not already published: every proposal above links the page that shows the gap it addresses.
  • Record the choice in COORDINATION.md (the shared board) so parallel sessions do not fork the decision.
