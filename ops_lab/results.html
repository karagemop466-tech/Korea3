Verified results feed

The brief requires a “verified results feed so backtests and ROI can be computed at all”. The feed holds one row per settled game × market × side with the final score, the market number, the price where published, and two independent outcomes side by side: one derived from the final score, one as published by the dataset's outcome column. Where both exist they are checked against each other on every build, and the published outcome is additionally re-derived from its own line.
