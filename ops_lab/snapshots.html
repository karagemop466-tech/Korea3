Price-snapshot store

The brief requires a store that “records opening and closing numbers for every market we originate”. This is that store: one row per (game × market × side × snapshot), copied verbatim from the pinned, hash-verified inputs. Where a source publishes no number for a market the book originates, the store holds an explicit blank row with the reason — so the question “do we hold a price for every market we originate?” is answered by rows, not by an assumption.

The NFL does not publish machine-readable betting prices, and neither the league nor an operator is the source here. All market numbers come from the pinned open dataset (nflverse/nfldata, tier 3, disclosed on every row). Within that dataset, true opening and closing pairs exist only where two snapshots exist: WSGT openers (2021, line only) versus closes (2006–2018). A single timestamped number is recorded as one snapshot and never labelled open or close.

What this store cannot do yet

honest limits, each with a path forward

  • No true open&to;close pair for 2019–2025 or for most of the 2026 book: closing_lines.csv stops at 2018 and initial_lines.csv stops after the 2021 season. The feed cannot derive closing-line value for those seasons; suggestions SG-ops1 and SG-ops8 propose the fix and need a source decision.
  • No book-identified prices: the dataset does not name the book behind spread_line/total_line, so the store records the number without a book claim.
  • The 2026 book is pin-time only: re-pinning (scheduled capture) is required before open&to;close movement becomes measurable on our own markets — SG-ops1.
