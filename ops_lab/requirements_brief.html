What was genuinely missing before this session

found by grep, closed additively

  • No demotion concept existed anywhere in the repository (grep across every page, generator and dataset) — built here: demotion gate ↗.
  • No results feed existed as a named, verifiable artifact — the settlement inputs existed but nothing joined, verified or published them — built here: results feed ↗.
  • No snapshot store existed; opening numbers (WSGT 2021) and closing numbers (2006–2018) sat in separate files with no store joining them per market — built here: snapshot store ↗.
  • The 2026 book originated no own-moneyline price — closed here as a labelled recomputation: origination order ↗.
  • The leaderboard mixed signal and staking in one ranking — separated here: signal / staking ↗.
