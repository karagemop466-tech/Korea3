Leaderboard, separated: signal vs staking

The brief requires the leaderboard to “separate ‘signal’ from ‘staking’ so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal”. The published leaderboard mixes both in one table. Here they are separated into two views computed from disjoint columns of the same bets — probability quality alone for signal, flat-stake cash flow alone for staking — and no combined rank is published. A staking hot streak cannot move the signal view; a well-calibrated loser cannot hide in the staking view.
