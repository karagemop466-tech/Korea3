Skill re-check — independent recomputation of the proper-score leaderboard

Requirement C03 ("rank with proper scores and uncertainty, not win rate") and C04 ("add a control baseline to every leaderboard run"), computed offline from the repository's pinned, checksummed artifacts. This is the independent recomputation layer: the merged skill_lab (PR #115) remains canonical on main and is not modified here — but its per-strategy skill numbers are invalid (see the reconciliation card below, flag L12), and the corrected leaderboard is the one on this page. The ranking metric is the Brier skill score against the de-vigged market probability with a cluster-bootstrap lower bound and Benjamini–Hochberg multiplicity control. Control baselines (coin-flip, majority side, seeded random) are shown with every run. Combos stay unranked behind the joint-evidence gate; the historical backtest ledger is kept visually and structurally separate.

Reconciliation — the merged skill_lab leaderboard (PR #115) scores only winning bets (flag L12)

Two sessions built this surface concurrently. The merged skill_lab publishes "5 of 8 strategies significantly better than the market". Its settled() filter keeps outcome in {0, 1}, but the ledger codes −1 = loss, 0 = push, +1 = win (line_lab/evaluation.py): every losing bet — 8,653 of 16,439 rows — is excluded from scoring and every push (296) is relabelled as a loss. Scoring only winning bets selects on the outcome, and the resulting "skill" is the strategies' edge restated. The table below reproduces their published numbers exactly under their own filter, then recomputes each strategy on the full settled set (losses included, pushes properly excluded): 0 of 8 beat the de-vigged market; every ranking bound is negative — the leaderboard below. Their controls (a different input file) are unaffected by this defect. Their files on main are canonical and are not modified here; a fix request is logged on the coordination board (flag L12).
