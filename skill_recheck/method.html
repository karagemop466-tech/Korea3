Method — proper-score skill ranking

Every rule below was fixed before any number was computed; the same text is the docstring of skill_recheck/core.py. Inputs are read-only pinned artifacts; nothing is fetched.

Inputs and identity

  • data/derived/bets.csv — the per-bet ledger (16,439 rows, S1–S11, line_lab build; SHA-256 0f9670527e9870328a079329cd6916c21fe1ac103cf3010aec996744eca557b1).
  • data/derived/summary.json — protocol constants (seed 20261101, evaluation window 2006–2025, FDR level 0.10) and the pinned nflverse commit a270278f3ac4e937295fe6a27814b3c4a2b62645.
  • data/raw/closing_lines.csv and data/raw/games.csv — the pinned third-party dataset used only for the global control universe and its settlement.
  • market_prob in the ledger is the proportionally de-vigged two-way closing probability (line_lab markets.devig_two_way); this lab re-derives nothing else about the market.

Rules (R1–R8)

  • R1 family. Ranked: singles S1–S8 (markets ATS, TOTAL, ML). S9/S10 parlays: unranked (flag L01, joint-evidence gate C01). S11 futures: unranked, no model probability (flag L02).
  • R2 scored subset. Rows with a model probability and a decided outcome (pushes excluded); every exclusion counted and published.
  • R3 score. Brier: BS = mean((p−y)²), y = 1 win / 0 loss. Benchmarks on the same events: market (de-vigged market probability of the same side), coin (0.5), favourite (majority-side probability on that event). BSS(x) = 1 − BS_model/BS_x.
  • R4 uncertainty. Cluster bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261119, two clusterings — game (game_id) and week (season, week). Numerator and denominator resampled together; the same resample sets are reused across benchmarks (paired). Reported per benchmark: 2.5/97.5 percentiles, one-sided p = P(BSS* ≤ 0), SE. Ranking bound = the more conservative (lower) of the two 2.5th percentiles; ranking p = the larger of the two one-sided p-values.
  • R5 multiplicity. Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.1 across the 8 ranked strategies on the ranking p-values; Bonferroni threshold 0.05/8 shown for reference. Eligibility: ranking bound > 0 AND q ≤ 0.1. No minimum-n rule — the bound carries the sample-size penalty.
  • R6 controls. Coin-flip and majority-side columns on each strategy's own events; global CONTROL rows in two labelled universes: A — events where the pinned closing-line file carries odds for both sides (moneylines 2006–2018; spread/total odds 2017–2018), majority side bet at the closing price with its de-vigged probability as forecast; B — every 2006–2025 game with a market line in the pinned modelling table, priced at the documented flat −110 fallback (100% imputed, flag L11), favourite-by-line and one seeded random realisation (flag L05). Controls are never ranked.
  • R7 what would change our mind. Confirm: additional bets at the observed BSS for the lower bound to reach 0, n = (z·SE/BSS)² (z = 1.645 one-sided), delta-method approximation. Overturn: BSS ≤ 1.645·SE on a prospective window drops the bound to ≤ 0.
  • R8 determinism. No wall clock anywhere; the build identity is the SHA-256 of the inputs. Two builds on the same inputs are byte-identical (checked by skill_recheck/check.py).

Settlement of the control universe

  • Spread: cover margin = side points + line − opponent points (>0 win, =0 push, <0 loss).
  • Total: over wins if total > line; push at equality; under the mirror.
  • Moneyline: more points wins; ties (two in the pinned window: 2008 PHI@CIN, 2012 STL@SF) push.
  • Cross-check: the dataset's own outcome column agrees with this recomputation on 20190 of 20208 recorded rows (99.91%) — every disagreement row is listed in skill_recheck/data/flags.json and flagged (L08). The modelling table's own score columns agree with games.csv on 5,199 of 5,199 joined games (flag L11 for its missing odds).

What this page does not claim

  • No official NFL number appears here: the dataset is the pinned nflverse commit (flag L04); the NFL publishes no closing lines, prices or probability forecasts.
  • Ranking is by skill only; ROI is shown in the separated backtest ledger and cannot promote a strategy (signal/staking split).
  • Pre-registration of the underlying strategies is line_lab's committed protocol; this lab adds scoring rules only (flag L09). Full pre-registration (A10) remains open on the readiness register.
