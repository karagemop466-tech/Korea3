Starters Out or Doubtful, and the next name the club published

A join of the clubs' own depth charts with the NFL's official Week 10 injury report, under one printed rule. Read the rule before the table.

The join rule, stated before the results. A row appears when the 1st-string player named by a club's own published depth chart also appears on the NFL's official Week 10 injury report for that club with a Game Status of Out or Doubtful. The replacement is the next name the same club published at the same position, in the club's own column order. Neither column is an official designation: no official publisher states who will start. Where the club published no further name, the replacement is blank and flagged.
What this table is not. It is not an official depth designation and it is not a prediction. No official publisher says who will start. The replacement column is literally the next name the club printed, which is a statement about what the club published, not about who will play.
