## Findings requiring follow-up

| ID / priority | Finding | Location and reason | Official-source context |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 — **high** | Settlement-rule evidence admission is too permissive. `EvidenceRegistry.audit_bet()` requires licensed evidence for the settlement record but only checks that `rules_id` exists; it does not require the rules record to be an official source, same publisher/operator, appropriate field, licensed/archived version, or hash. A link-only or wrong-publisher rules entry can therefore be accepted by this method. **Reproduced with a synthetic, non-NFL fixture:** `audit_bet()` accepted link-only NFL Terms as `rules_id` for a DraftKings settlement with a nonexistent quote ID. | `nfl_lab/evidence.py`, `audit_bet`; called by `nfl_lab/run.py` and `nfl_lab/validation.py`. Require and validate the matching operator's date/jurisdiction-specific rules evidence before accepting a settled record. | [DraftKings' player-settlement guidance directs users to sport-specific house rules](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/how-will-my-bet-be-settled-if-a-player-doesn-t-start-or-is-removed-from-a?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010795); [DraftKings market rules](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/help/general-betting-rules/market-rules) |
| R2 — **high** | The settlement admission path does not resolve `quote_id` to an admitted quote or prove the same event/market/side/threshold/operator/timestamp/executable price; `SettledBet` carries only a quote ID and `audit_bet()` does not consume the quote registry. `executable_quotes_verified` is an external boolean parameter, not a chain-of-evidence check. | `nfl_lab/contracts.py` (`SettledBet`); `nfl_lab/evidence.py` (`audit_bet`); `nfl_lab/validation.py` (`evaluate_strategy`, `execution_and_risk`). Before any future admission, link each settled record to a quote object and verify all identifiers and timing/rules. | [DraftKings explains odds as offered quote/payout terms](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/understanding-odds?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010466); [its market rules are operator-specific](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/help/general-betting-rules/market-rules) |
| R3 — **high** | Forecast evidence and provenance are not tied to settlement rows in the validation call. Forecasts receive chronology checks, while probability scoring reads `p_win`/`baseline_p_win` stored on bets; the evaluator does not establish that the scored model probabilities are the registered forecasts for the same event, market, selection, and pregame snapshot. | `nfl_lab/validation.py`, `evaluate_strategy`. | R4 — **medium** | The site checker guarantees a link from an official host, not that the source ID/URL is a correct semantic match for every row. Also, the checker does not re-fetch sources, so source-page edits or dead links after review are not detected by CI. | `nfl_lab/check_verified.py`. Add a row-level `evidence_id` to generated table data, assert its URL equals the registry URL, and maintain a scheduled/manual content re-review. | [NFL Terms page notes that terms can change](https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms/); [DraftKings pages expose revision dates, e.g. odds](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/understanding-odds?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010466) |
