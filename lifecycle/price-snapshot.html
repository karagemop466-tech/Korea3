Opening and closing numbers for every market we originate

A store that records, for the side, the total and the moneyline, the opening and closing numbers as separate columns. The schema and the market definitions are cited; every price is blank and flagged, because no official publisher releases one.

The rule, stated before the tables. Opening and closing are stored as two separate rows per selection, never merged into one. A snapshot kind with no official value is stored as a blank row with verified=false and a flag, so the store shows what is missing rather than substituting a number.
Every price here is blank, and that is the finding. The NFL publishes no machine-readable line and no licensed operator publishes an opening or closing archive this project can cite. The pinned open dataset is the only line source and it is Tier 3. A blank cell is the honest record of an unpublished value, not a value to fill.

Lifecycle defines the bookkeeping around a strategy: what price was recorded when a market opened and closed, what the settled result feed contains, the rule that retires a live strategy, and the separation of signal from staking. Every value is either an official-publisher fact, a value recomputed from a committed file and labelled as such, or a blank that is flagged for review.
