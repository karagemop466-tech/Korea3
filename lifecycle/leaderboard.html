Signal and staking, kept as two separate leaderboards

A signal leaderboard and a staking leaderboard, computed independently and never blended, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal. Both are recomputed from the committed strategy ledger.

The separation, stated before the tables. Two registers are computed independently and are never blended into one rank. The signal register ranks by statistical signal quality (ascending BH q, then descending shrunk ROI); the staking register ranks by realized return on a flat 1-unit stake. A strategy can rank high on staking while ranking low on signal. Blending the two would let a lucky staking run mask a bad signal, which is exactly what the brief rules out.
