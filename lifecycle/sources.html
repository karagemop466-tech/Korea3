Sources

Where every value on this site came from, and how each publisher is classified.

Tier definitions are this build's own, recorded rather than assumed. A source is entered here only if the page was opened and read during this session 2026-11-14. Official league and licensed-operator pages are primary for their facts; the government statistical pages are method references and endorse no strategy; the committed artifacts are labelled recomputed wherever a value derives from them; the pinned dataset is Tier 3 because the NFL publishes no machine-readable lines.

Lifecycle defines the bookkeeping around a strategy: what price was recorded when a market opened and closed, what the settled result feed contains, the rule that retires a live strategy, and the separation of signal from staking. Every value is either an official-publisher fact, a value recomputed from a committed file and labelled as such, or a blank that is flagged for review.
