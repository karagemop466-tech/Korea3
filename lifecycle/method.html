Method and limits

How the structures were defined and computed, and what they cannot tell you.

How this was made

These are this build's own actions, not sourced facts, so they are written out rather than tabulated.

5 steps
1. Claim the scope
A block was appended to COORDINATION.md naming the session, the file scope and the tasks, and pushed before any scoped file was touched.
2. Read the official sources
The DraftKings odds and market-rules pages, the two NFL Support articles, and the two NIST statistical pages were opened and read on 2026-11-14.
3. Define the structures
The price-snapshot store, the results feed and the demotion gate were specified as schemas, with their rules written before any result.
4. Compute deterministically
lifecycle/gate.py reads the committed ledgers and writes the demotion evaluation, both leaderboards and the feed summary. No network, no invented value.
5. Build and fail closed
lifecycle/build.py refuses to emit a row without a source link; lifecycle/check.py fails the build if a page links a non-HTTPS or non-registered host, a page is missing, or a number disagrees with the data.

Limits

Read these before using anything on this site.

6 limits
No market price is published
The NFL publishes no machine-readable line and no licensed operator publishes an opening or closing archive this project can cite. Every price cell is blank and flagged.
The results feed is Tier 3
The settled bet stream comes from a pinned third-party-derived dataset. It records outcomes at recorded prices; it is not a licensed operator ledger and supports no skill claim.
The demotion gate is a design, not a sourced fact
Its parameters are this build's own choices, mirroring the repository's promotion gates. The method sources cited are statistical references, not endorsements.
The leaderboards are recomputed, not live
Both registers are deterministic functions of the committed ledger. They describe the committed record, not a live strategy.
No staking plan is evaluated
Every bet is a flat 1-unit stake. The staking register ranks realized return; it does not model any staking system.
The NFL Support bodies are swapped
The money-line and point-spread articles serve each other's text. Both readings are recorded rather than one being trusted.

Lifecycle defines the bookkeeping around a strategy: what price was recorded when a market opened and closed, what the settled result feed contains, the rule that retires a live strategy, and the separation of signal from staking. Every value is either an official-publisher fact, a value recomputed from a committed file and labelled as such, or a blank that is flagged for review.
