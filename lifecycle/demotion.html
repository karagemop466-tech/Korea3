The rule that sends a live strategy back to paper

A demotion gate: a fixed predicate on trailing evidence that retires a live strategy to paper when the evidence no longer supports live status. The rule is printed before any result, and the evaluation is recomputed from the committed strategy ledger.

The demotion rule, stated before the results. A strategy is LIVE only if it clears every trigger below. The first trigger that fires, in the fixed order N, Q, CI, S, G, is the recorded reason; every trigger that fires is listed. A strategy that fires any trigger is DEMOTED — returned to paper — and is not ranked for rewards.

Lifecycle defines the bookkeeping around a strategy: what price was recorded when a market opened and closed, what the settled result feed contains, the rule that retires a live strategy, and the separation of signal from staking. Every value is either an official-publisher fact, a value recomputed from a committed file and labelled as such, or a blank that is flagged for review.
