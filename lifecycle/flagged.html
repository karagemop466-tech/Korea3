Flagged for review

The blanks, the limits of what is computable, and the official source that contradicts itself. Every row links its source.

Why this page exists. The brief says a value that cannot be confirmed from an official source must be left blank and flagged, never inferred. Everything here is a limit of what is computable, an official source staying silent, or an official source contradicting itself. None of it is smoothed over, and none of it blocks the build.

Lifecycle defines the bookkeeping around a strategy: what price was recorded when a market opened and closed, what the settled result feed contains, the rule that retires a live strategy, and the separation of signal from staking. Every value is either an official-publisher fact, a value recomputed from a committed file and labelled as such, or a blank that is flagged for review.
