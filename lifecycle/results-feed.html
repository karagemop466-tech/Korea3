What the feed is not

The feed is a record of settled bets at recorded prices. It is not a licensed operator's settlement ledger and it is not a source of market prices: the prices in it come from the pinned open dataset and are Tier 3. The feed is what makes an ROI arithmetic computable at all; it does not make any ROI claim about skill. No price is imputed into a row that lacks one.

Lifecycle defines the bookkeeping around a strategy: what price was recorded when a market opened and closed, what the settled result feed contains, the rule that retires a live strategy, and the separation of signal from staking. Every value is either an official-publisher fact, a value recomputed from a committed file and labelled as such, or a blank that is flagged for review.
