What this computes. For every pre-registered strategy, the benchmark-relative Brier skill score (BSS) measures prediction skill against the closing line: BSS = Brier(model_prob, outcome) − Brier(market_prob, outcome), over settled bets (pushes excluded from Brier and counted separately as n push). Uncertainty is a game-cluster bootstrap — outcomes are resampled by (season, week) cluster, 2000 replicates, giving a 95% percentile interval and a one-sided p-value (skill > 0). Multiplicity is controlled with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR across the ranked strategies, with Bonferroni published alongside. Controls are computed on the verified results-feed universe: the market benchmark (closing implied probability), random 50/50 picks (analytic Brier 0.25), and favourites-only (always the higher-implied-probability side).

Why skill, not win rate

Raw win rate and short-run ROI cannot separate skill from variance. A strategy can post a positive ROI over a season purely by luck; the game-cluster bootstrap and the multiplicity adjustment are what make a skill claim testable. A strategy is only ranked here if it has a computable model_prob; parlays are computed but held behind the joint-evidence gate (C01), and strategies with no probability in the pinned data are left blank and flagged.

Relationship to the live leaderboard. Every number on this surface is a backtest skill metric computed over the historical bet universe. Readiness requirement A09 ("forbid backtest numbers from ever appearing on the leaderboard") is still open: the live promotion leaderboard must not carry these figures until A09 is satisfied. This page is the skill-evaluation layer, published separately so skill and luck can be judged without polluting the live board.
