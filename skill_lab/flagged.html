L01 · Parlay probabilities are products of leg marginals; combos stay unranked

high open

S9/S10 model_prob values in bets.csv are np.prod of leg probabilities (line_lab/strategies.py _add_parlays). Under dependence, the product is not a joint probability, so the joint-evidence gate (C01) is not satisfied and the combos are published unranked with skill columns blank.

Sources: strategies.py · bets.csv

L04 · Benchmarks are the repository's pinned third-party dataset, not official league data

L08 · Pinned closing-lines outcome column contradicts pinned scores on 18 rows

high open

Recomputing settlement from data/raw/closing_lines.csv (line, odds) joined to data/raw/games.csv (scores) disagrees with the outcome column of closing_lines.csv itself on 6 SPREAD, 8 TOTAL and 4 MONEYLINE rows (of 20208 with a recorded outcome). Moneyline ties recorded as 0.5 are pushes and are treated as such; the remaining rows flip the recorded winner of the bet. This lab settles control bets from the pinned scores and publishes the agreement rate; the affected rows are listed in skill_lab/data/flags.json (L08.mismatch_rows). The line_lab ledger settles from scores too and is not affected.

Sources: closing_lines.csv · games.csv

L11 · Control universe B prices are 100% imputed at the documented -110 fallback

medium open

data/raw/closing_lines.csv carries closing odds only for part of the window (moneylines throughout 2006-2018; spread/total odds only 2017-2018), so universe A is restricted to priced events and published as such. To give the favourite control full-window coverage, universe B prices every bet at the flat -110 fallback documented in research/methodology.md. Its ROI is therefore a standard-vig anchor, not a market-price outcome, and no de-vigged forecast exists (Brier columns blank).

Sources: methodology.md · closing_lines.csv · model_predictions.csv
