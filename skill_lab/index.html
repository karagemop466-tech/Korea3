This page ranks NFL line-origination strategies by prediction skill, not win rate or ROI. The headline metric is the benchmark-relative Brier skill score (BSS): how much better (or worse) a strategy predicts the side it bet on than the closing line's own implied probability, with a game-cluster bootstrap confidence interval and a multiplicity-adjusted significance test. Two no-skill control baselines — random 50/50 picks and favourites-only — are published beside every strategy so skill can be separated from luck. Every value carries its sample size n; nothing is inferred.
Scope note: these are backtest skill metrics computed over the historical bet universe. Requirement A09 (forbid backtest numbers from the live promotion leaderboard) remains open and is tracked on the readiness register; this surface is the separate skill-evaluation layer, not the live leaderboard.
