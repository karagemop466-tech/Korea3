Official availability capture

A scheduled, dated record of the NFL injury report and every club depth-chart page. Each capture keeps the raw body's SHA-256, the UTC fetch time and the status, so a later reading can be replayed against what was published when. A page that stops matching its expected marker is recorded as a failure, not as a blank.

Schedule: .github/workflows/capture.yml. Manifest: capture/data/manifest.jsonl.
