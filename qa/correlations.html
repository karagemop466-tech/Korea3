Correlation: what is measured, and what is not reproducible

The arithmetic behind the threshold

At −110 each leg is 1.90909 decimal. A two-leg parlay pays 3.6446, so the break-even joint probability is 1/3.6446 = 0.27438. For two fair coins P(both win) = 0.25 + φ/4, so the dependence needed is φ = 4 × (0.27438 − 0.25) = 0.09751. Nothing measured here is close: published values span +0.0086 to +0.020, and this session's independent recomputations span −0.0085 to −0.0039.
Point estimates disagree; conclusions do not. The three published φ values could not be reproduced from the committed raw file under any filter tried, and one of them has the opposite sign to every recomputation here. All of them, published and recomputed, sit within ±0.025 of zero. Treat the point estimates as unverified (F-06) and the conclusion "no usable spread/total dependence" as supported.
