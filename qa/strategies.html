Why the gate counts are the real result

A rule can look profitable and still be worthless. The gates exist to separate "returned money" from "predicted money": beat the closing line, survive a holdout, survive a multiplicity correction, survive a no-skill bootstrap, and stay stable across seasons. Across all three strategy inventories (11 + 18 + 28 rules) exactly zero do all of that. That is the headline, and this session verified the arithmetic behind it.
