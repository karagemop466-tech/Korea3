Executive summary

One page for the whole project: own NFL lines, 29 tested strategies across two pre-registered families, three gate systems, measured correlations, parlay arithmetic, a player-prop framework with honestly blank values, and a leaderboard that has correctly paid nobody. Every number here was independently recomputed on 2026-11-02 (66/66 checks pass).

Bottom line. Origination works as research; nothing tested works as edge. The own line loses to the close by 0.42 pts/game, the best raw signal (+8.41%, 607 bets) dies on multiplicity, same-game spread×total correlation is zero where a parlay needs φ ≥ 0.0975, and the official-evidence layer publishes zero verified values because no licensed dataset exists. That is the finding — measurement, not picks. Full text: CONSOLIDATED_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md ↗
