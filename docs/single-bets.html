Strategies

Single-bet strategies

Pricing rule. Every bet is settled against the official final score in the dataset and priced at the closing price carried in the dataset (home/away_spread_odds, over/under_odds, moneylines), falling back to the standard -110 only where the dataset carries no price. Pushes settle at 0 units and stay in the denominator.
