Own lines — origination model and market comparison

The model — what "our own line" means here

Spread: model_margin = b0 + b1 × (Elo_home − Elo_away), where Elo is a margin-of-victory Elo rating updated after every game with 1/3 regression to the mean between seasons. Total: model_total = c0 + c1 × projected_total + weather/dome adjustments, where projected points come from exponentially weighted team scoring and conceding rates. Coefficients are estimated on the design window only (1999–2018, n=5,316) and applied forward.

Opening versus closing line — 2021 only

Caveat. Opening lines exist for a single season and a single book (data/initial_lines.csv (book: WSGT) vs closing spread in data/games_1999_2026_nflverse.csv). The measured mean absolute gap of 9.653 points is far larger than a normal open-to-close move, so this snapshot behaves like an early/lookahead line rather than a same-week open. It is published for completeness and is not used to claim closing-line value (F-05).
