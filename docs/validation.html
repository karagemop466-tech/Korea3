Validation Gates

Nine pre-registered filters, and which strategies survive each one.

A strategy is only "validated" if it survives all nine gates. They are deliberately hostile: sample size, coverage, leakage, out-of-sample holdout, a zero-skill simulation, multiple-testing correction, season consistency, robustness to dropping the best season, and whether the prices were actually observed.
