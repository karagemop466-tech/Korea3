Methodology

Everything needed to reproduce every number on this site, and every place where the method could be wrong.

Pipeline

  1. scripts/fetch_data.sh — downloads the dataset at a pinned commit. Byte-identical for anyone who runs it.
  2. scripts/build_dataset.py — builds the modelling table, enforces the sign convention, drops unplayed games, reconciles margins and totals.
  3. scripts/model.py — walk-forward ratings model; outputs own lines for every game and the accuracy tables.
  4. scripts/blend.py — information test and out-of-sample blend.
  5. scripts/signals.py — the 18-rule family and its backtests.
  6. scripts/gates.py — the eight gates, FDR control, empirical-Bayes shrinkage, leaderboard order.
  7. scripts/correlation.py, scripts/props.py — correlation lab and the prop framework.
  8. scripts/own_lines.py — own lines for unplayed fixtures.
  9. scripts/build_site.py, scripts/verify_site.py — generate and verify this site.

Data provenance

Market numbers come from the open nflverse/nfldata dataset, pinned at commit a270278f3ac4e937295fe6a27814b3c4a2b62645 dated 2026-11-01T13:05:15Z. The NFL does not publish lines, so no official league source exists for them; the dataset is disclosed as Tier 3 in the source registry and every row links to the exact file. Rules, scoring, overtime, tie handling, break-even pricing and push behaviour are cited to official NFL and licensed-operator sources.

Statistical methods (stated, not tuned)

  • Break-even at −110: 110/210 = 52.3810%. Cited to the NFL's own explanation of point-spread pricing.
  • Confidence intervals: percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261101.
  • Windows: DISCOVERY 2000–2015, CONFIRMATION 2016–2026. Fixed before evaluation.
  • Multiplicity: Benjamini–Hochberg across all 18 rules, q ≤ 0.1.
  • Shrinkage: moment-matched empirical Bayes, τ² = max(0, Var(observed edges) − mean(SE²)) = 0.000636.
  • Model tuning: λ = 4.0 and half-life 365 days fixed a priori; the nine-setting sensitivity grid is reported so a reader can see the conclusion does not depend on it.
  • Push handling: excluded from win-rate and ROI, counted separately. Spread push rate 2.76%, total push rate 1.40%.

Known limitations (stated plainly)

  • No injury or inactive data. This is the largest omission in the feature set and probably in the results.
  • No lineup or snap-count data, so no player-level research could be done at all.
  • Prices imputed at −110 for 26.9% of games. Real prices are used where recorded; the imputation is flagged per rule.
  • Two line sources disagree — the primary spread matches the per-side closing file exactly on 67.3% of overlapping games. Gate G7 exists because of this.
  • One book's line is not every book's line. A strategy that is profitable only against a particular book's number is not a strategy.
  • Survivorship in the dataset: the dataset is complete season by season, so this is not an issue here — but future scrapes must be checked for it.
  • No closing-line-value study before 2021 because opening lines are only available from 2021 in this dataset.
