Reading the table

  • Multiplicity is the whole point: scanning 56 rules and keeping the best one is how backtests lie. Benjamini-Hochberg at 5% across the full battery is the correction applied here.
  • The single survivor (wind ≥ 12 mph → under) is negative in the 2024–2026 holdout, which is what an era-specific artefact looks like.
  • Weather rows are computed on the 71% of games with weather data and exclude domes by construction — see F-06.
