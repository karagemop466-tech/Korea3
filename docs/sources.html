Verification policy

  • No number on this site came from memory. Each one is either computed by a script from data/ or transcribed from an official page into data/official/ with its URL stored beside it.
  • Where an official page could not be reached, the value is blank and appears on Flagged for review. Nothing was inferred to fill a gap.
  • The dataset itself is cross-checked against the league: 2025 and 2026 W/L/T/PF/PA for all 32 teams, 64/64 rows matching (Data & verification).
  • Transcriptions deliberately exclude anything that could be misread (playoff glyphs, streaks, badges) — see F-01.

No value in this project is typed in from memory or inferred. Every number on the site is either (a) computed by pipeline/ scripts from the CSV files in data/, or (b) transcribed from an official nfl.com page and stored next to its source_url in data/official/. Transcriptions from nfl.com contain only W/L/T/PF/PA. Playoff-seeding glyphs and streaks shown on the page were deliberately not transcribed.

Provenance. Every figure on this page is generated by pipeline/ from the files in data/. Values that could not be confirmed from a primary source are left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page.

Data: nflverse/nfldata (game results and closing lines derived from official NFL gamebooks), independently re-verified against nfl.com official standings. This is research tooling, not betting advice.
