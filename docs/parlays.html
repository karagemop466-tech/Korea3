What this says. Spread and total legs are close to independent in this data set (phi ≈ 0.02, CI spanning zero) — the classic "game script" story does not show up at these price points. The dependence that matters is within the same team's result: winning outright and covering the spread are the same event most of the time (phi ≈ 0.67). Team points across opponents are nearly independent (−0.03), which is why game totals are only mildly more variable than the sum of two independent team scores.

Do not read the big EV numbers as free money. Real sportsbooks price same-game parlays with a correlation model, restrict or reject the most correlated combinations, and shade the price. This table measures the size of the pricing error an independent-multiplication book would make; it is not an offer, and no strategy in this project bets it (see F-07).
