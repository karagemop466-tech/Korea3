Headline findings

  • Verification first. All 64 official standings rows (2025 and 2026 partial season) match the dataset exactly, covering 380 games. Nothing downstream is built on unchecked scores.
  • Own lines are real but not yet competitive. Our spread model's mean absolute error is 10.67 points versus the market's 10.44 in the design window and 10.17 vs 9.63 in the holdout.
  • Luck is measurable. 56 mechanical signals were scanned; 1 survives Benjamini-Hochberg and only 4 are positive in both out-of-sample windows (≈2.8 false positives would be expected by chance alone).
  • Wind is the one live candidate family. Wind ≥ 12 mph → under: n=1,304, ROI +8.5% full sample, but -7.0% in the 2024–2026 holdout — on probation, not funded.
  • Parlay correlation is where the structure lives. phi(home ML, home cover) = 0.6721 versus phi(cover, total) ≈ 0.0159. Independent-multiplication pricing of same-game legs is wrong by tens of percent of stake — but books already price correlation (F-07).
  • Zero strategies qualified for reward units. The leaderboard pays only gate-complete entries; the current best is S08 at 4/7 gates.

Every statement above links to the page that computes it: verification, own lines, signal research, parlay correlations, leaderboard.

What this project is

  • A verified data pipeline: ingestion, provenance, and independent re-verification against nfl.com.
  • A line-origination model (Elo-based spread, volume/weather-based total) with honest accuracy reporting.
  • A signal-research lab: whole families scanned over threshold grids, with multiplicity control and design/validation/holdout separation.
  • A validation-gate system and a reward leaderboard that only pays gate-complete strategies.
  • A correlation engine that measures, rather than assumes, how same-game legs co-move.

What it is not

  • Not a tipster service and not betting advice.
  • Not a place where a number appears without a source or a script.
  • Not a claim that any strategy is profitable: the honest result is that none passes all gates.
  • Not complete on player props — that data could not be verified here (see F-04).
