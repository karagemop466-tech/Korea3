Validation gates

Design principle. A gate is only useful if it can fail. These gates are applied to every strategy, in public, and the result here is that nothing passes — which is the honest output of a scan over 56 rules and 15 composites on 1999–2026 data.

Gate definitions

GateNameRequirementWhy it exists
G1SAMPLE≥ 300 settled bets overall and ≥ 60 in the 2024–2026 holdoutSmall samples win leaderboards by accident. This floor makes the tail risk of a lucky streak explicit.
G2ROI_POSITIVEPositive ROI overall AND positive ROI in the holdoutA rule that only worked in the fitting window is a description of history, not an edge.
G3SIGNIFICANCEBenjamini-Hochberg adjusted one-sided p < 0.05 across all 15 strategiesControlling false discovery across the whole battery is what separates a signal from a scan artefact.
G4STABILITY≥ 55% of qualified seasons positive AND no single season > 50% of total profitOne monster season is how luck looks in a P&L. The gate forces the profit to be spread.
G5DRAWDOWNMax peak-to-trough drawdown ≤ 30 unitsCapacity and bankroll survive drawdowns, not ROI. A strategy that cannot be held is not a strategy.
G6CLVMean closing-line value ≥ 0 where opening lines existBeating the close is the strongest available evidence of skill rather than luck - but the verified data only has a 2021 opening snapshot, so this gate reports n/a here (F-05) and never counts as a pass.
G7HOLDOUT_NOT_NEGATIVEHoldout ROI 95% CI lower bound > −10%Even the lower bound of the unseen-data estimate must not imply a blow-up.

Removing luck — the four mechanisms used

  • Time separation. Coefficients are fit on 1999–2018 and never touched again; 2019–2023 is validation, 2024–2026 is a holdout reported for every rule including the winners.
  • Multiplicity control. 56 tests, Benjamini-Hochberg at 5%. With no real signal you would expect ≈2.8 survivors; we found 1.
  • Distribution, not point estimates. Every ROI carries a t-statistic, a 95% CI and a max drawdown.
  • Pre-registered thresholds. Strategy rules and thresholds are fixed in pipeline/ before results are read, and the scan reports every threshold, not the winner.

Provenance. Every figure on this page is generated by pipeline/ from the files in data/. Values that could not be confirmed from a primary source are left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page.
