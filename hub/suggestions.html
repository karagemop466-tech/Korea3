How to use this page

Rows are ordered by dependency, not by excitement. Effort, risk and recommendation are this session's judgement — they are not facts and they link to the official basis behind the problem, not to a source that would prove the opinion. Each row names the questions from the register that it would close, so you can see what a choice buys before you make it.

Pick a path, record it on the questions page, and only then implement. That is the review gate the brief asked for.

Recommended path, in order

Six steps. The first three cost nothing and two of them lose evidence every week they are delayed.

Ordering is a recommendation from this session; it is not a verified fact.
Step Action Why now Gated by Official source
1 S06 — pre-register the hypothesis family, sample size and holdout rule. It costs nothing and must exist before any data is seen, otherwise every later result is suspect. nothing REPO ↗
2 S02 + S03 — start the weekly official injury and inactive archive. Snapshots can only be collected forward in time; every week of delay is a week of evidence permanently lost. nothing REPO ↗
3 S08 + S07 + S09 — precedence rule, decision log, known/unknown ledger. Makes the rest of the work auditable while it is still cheap to change. nothing REPO ↗

6 S10 — reward terms, or publish the leaderboard as notional. Ranking without stakes is fine; claiming a payout without funding is not. owner decision (Q08) REPO ↗

S01 License a timestamped executable-quote archive (one vendor, one contract) and store it immutably with checksums. No measured value can be produced: not ROI, edge, hold, correlation or own-line error. Unblocks the leaderboard, the correlation page and own-line accuracy in one step, and every number becomes reproducible. High (contract, cost, legal review). Vendor lock-in; terms may forbid redistribution, so only derived aggregates could be published. Q01, Q10, Q11 Pilot first — highest value per unit of work NFL-TERMS ↗ DK-MARKETS ↗ REPO ↗
S02 Archive the official weekly injury page and every club report exactly as published, with a fetch timestamp and a checksum, every week. The injury view is a point-in-time read: today's page overwrites last week's, so nothing can be back-tested. Builds the only lawful path to Q03 and Q07; makes every injury table row reproducible and re-checkable. Medium (scheduled job, storage). The official page's season selector reaches back to 1965, but only snapshots taken now are provably what was shown. Q03, Q18 Adopt now NFL-INJURIES ↗ NFL-INJ-POLICY ↗ REPO ↗
S03 Capture the official inactive list for every game and join it to the game-status labels. 'Questionable' and blank statuses cannot be converted into realised availability. Turns qualitative labels into countable rates and validates or kills the injury signal. Medium. The official inactives page currently prints 'Please check back soon for NFL Inactive Reports for this Season', so coverage may be incomplete. Q03, Q07 Adopt now, with a coverage flag per week NFL-INACTIVES ↗ REPO ↗
S04 Keep club depth charts as labelled ordinal context only, printing each club's own disclaimer next to the data. Depth ordinals are easy to misread as official starter designations. Prevents the most likely false claim on the site ('backup X starts for injured Y'). Low (already implemented on the new availability page). Two of four clubs explicitly call their chart unofficial, so ordinals cannot enter a model as a feature. Q05, Q17 Adopt now CLUB-NE-DEPTH ↗ CLUB-DET-DEPTH ↗ REPO ↗
S05 License official participation and snap-count data to measure the next-man-up effect. The question 'what does losing a starter cost on the line?' cannot be answered from depth charts. Converts the depth ledger from description into measurement, and lets position importance be estimated instead of assumed. High. Without pre-registration this becomes an unconstrained search; it must be gated before any data arrives. Q04, Q17 Defer until S01 and S06 are in place NFL-STATS ↗ NIST-MULTI ↗ REPO ↗
S06 Pre-register the next-man-up hypothesis family with a fixed sample-size and holdout rule before any data arrives. Promotion decisions have no power calculation, so 'skill' cannot be separated from noise. Makes the leaderboard meaningful and the luck claim testable. Low (writing, no data needed). A registered family that later proves untestable must be publicly retired, not quietly dropped. Q13, Q14, Q16 Adopt now NIST-CI ↗ NIST-BONF ↗ REPO ↗
S07 Publish a dated decision log: every suggestion, who accepted it, and what was rejected and why. The brief asks for a review path before implementation, and a review is only auditable if the decision is written down. Removes ambiguity about what was chosen and why; feeds the executive summary. Low. A log that is not enforced drifts; the build gate must fail if a decision row is missing a reason. Q12, Q15 Adopt now REPO ↗ REPO ↗
S08 Adopt a written source-precedence rule and a conflict register for cases where two official pages disagree. Observed conflicts (jersey numbers; players absent from depth charts) have no resolution rule. Every future join becomes deterministic and reviewable. Low. Precedence can hide a real error in the 'winning' source; the conflict must still be published. Q06, Q19 Adopt now NFL-ROSTER-WAS ↗ CLUB-WAS-ROSTER ↗ REPO ↗
S09 Put the known/unknown ledger on the executive summary page, not only on a research page. Decision-makers read the summary first; the caveats currently sit deeper in the site. Stops a reader taking a blank as a zero. Low. Adds length to the summary; needs a strict one-screen limit. Q03, Q08 Adopt now REPO ↗ REPO ↗
S10 Write the reward terms as a specification with an escrow or nothing at all. A leaderboard with unspecified rewards is a ranking with no stakes, and any payout claim would be unsupported. Makes the competition real or explicitly notional. Low to specify, high to fund. Funding a competition on unmeasured strategies rewards luck; terms must require gates to pass first. Q08, Q15 Requires owner decision — options listed, nothing implemented NFL-RG ↗ REPO ↗ REPO ↗
S11 Reject systematic scraping of NFL.com pages as a data-acquisition route. The cheapest data path is also the one the league prohibits. Keeps the project lawful and defensible; forces a licensed or permissioned route. None to reject. Slows delivery, which is the correct trade here. Q02 Reject — do not implement NFL-TERMS ↗ REPO ↗
S12 Reject inferring injury severity or return timelines from practice participation. A 'limited practice' is not a severity score and not a probability of playing. Prevents fabricated numbers entering the model and the site. None to reject. Loses an apparent signal that the official definitions do not support. Q03, Q07 Reject — do not implement NFL-INJ-POLICY ↗ REPO ↗
S13 Apply a NIST-documented multiplicity adjustment and publish the adjusted threshold with every result. Testing many strategies guarantees a flattering winner by chance. Makes 'top performing' mean something after correction. Low. Over-correction can hide a real edge in a small family; the family size must be honest. Q09 Adopt now NIST-MULTI ↗ NIST-BONF ↗ NIH-FDR ↗ REPO ↗
S14 Add a scheduled re-check of every external link and every dated claim to CI. Official pages move: three injury-policy URLs and the NFL.com depth-chart path were 404 today. Turns a stale link into a build failure instead of a published error. Low. Network-dependent CI is flaky; failures must be reported, not auto-fixed. Q20 Adopt now NFL-TERMS ↗ REPO ↗ REPO ↗
