What this page can and cannot say

The NFL publishes a weekly injury report with practice and game status. It does not publish a depth chart, a starter designation or a snap count. Depth charts come from the clubs, in four different layouts, and two of the four read here are explicitly labelled unofficial by the club itself. So this page can state, with a link, who is listed as injured and who the club lists next at that position. It cannot state that a player is officially the starter, and it does not: every depth reading is marked recomputed, and the five players who appear on an injury report but on no depth row are flagged rather than assigned a role.

Three official pages answer the three structural questions.

How this feeds a strategy — and what it cannot do yet

An availability signal would enter the same fail-closed pipeline as every other rule: a pre-registered definition, an untouched holdout, multiplicity control and a price-based test. None of that is possible from four clubs and one week, so nothing here is offered as a signal.
