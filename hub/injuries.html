What is official here, and what is not

Three separate things are being published, and they must not be confused. Game status and practice status are official: they are the NFL's own columns with the NFL's own vocabulary. The availability-stress index is this repository's arithmetic on top of those official values, with the weights printed in full below so anyone can redo it. Depth order — who is second or third string — is not official here and is left blank.

Layer What it is Source Status Official source
Game status Out = will not play; Doubtful = unlikely; Questionable = uncertain; blank = the club has not designated the player. source ↗ source ↗
Practice status Did Not Participate; Limited Participation (less than 100% of normal repetitions); Full Participation (100%). source ↗ source ↗
Availability-stress index 0-5 per player. A publishing convention of this repository, not an NFL metric. Weights below. repo ↗ source ↗
Depth / string 2nd, 3rd and 4th string. Not available: the official depth-chart URL returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-12. source ↗ source ↗

The index, with its arithmetic shown

Each listed player scores game-status weight + practice weight. Game status: Out 3, Doubtful 2, Questionable 1, not designated 0. Practice: 2 for did not participate, 1 for limited, 0 for full. A player ruled Out after missing practice therefore scores 5; a player practising fully with no designation scores 0. The index measures reported unavailability, not player quality and not depth.

Why four clubs have no rows

Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay do not appear on the Week 10 injury report. The official week 10 schedule page lists exactly those four clubs under Teams on Bye, so the absence is explained by an official source and is recorded as 'absent from page' rather than as 'no injuries'. Two further clubs, Seattle and Cincinnati, appear on the report with the literal text 'No Injuries Reported'.
