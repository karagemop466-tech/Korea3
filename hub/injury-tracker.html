Injury tracker & lineup deficiency analysis

A framework for tracking NFL injuries and their impact on team performance and betting lines. This page documents what data is needed, how injuries affect defensive and offensive units at different depth levels (1st through 4th string), and what line adjustments are expected. Most impact values are unverified estimates pending licensed data; they are clearly labelled and linked to the review page.

Framework overview

The injury tracker operates in three layers: (1) data inputs — the injury report and depth chart data; (2) impact analysis — how each injury affects the team's expected performance; (3) line adjustment — how the expected performance change translates to a line movement. Every layer requires data that is not yet available in an automated, official feed.

This is the primary input. The report is machine-readable only as HTML/PDF — no official API exists. nfl.com/injuries (per team) NFL-STATS ↗ NFL-GAMEDAY ↗

Third-party providers (FantasyLabs, Rotoworld) maintain depth charts. Critical for knowing who replaces a starter. Defensive injury impact — by position group

How injuries at each defensive position affect expected scoring and betting lines. Impact estimates are based on football analysis principles and have not been validated against historical data in this repository.

Impact estimates are analytical, not empirically verified. All require validation against historical data.
ID Position Impact mechanism Expected line movement Source links Official source
DI01 CB1 (Starting Cornerback 1) Shutdown corner removed → opposing WR1 runs free. Expected impact: +3-6 pts for opposing offense. If CB2 also out, impact doubles. CB3 available: moderate mitigation. No CB2 or CB3: severe. Defensive backfield injuries have the largest per-player impact on scoring. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ NFL-GAMEDAY ↗
DI02 Pass Rush (DE/OLB starters) Losing a premier pass rusher reduces pressure rate by ~5-8%. Without pressure, QB has more time, completions increase, scoring goes up. Affects both ATS and totals. Pass rush injuries shift the total upward and may move the spread 1-2 points. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
DI03 Linebacker corps (ILB/OLB) Run defense degrades with backup linebackers. Affects rushing yards and short-yardage situations. Impact on totals is moderate; impact on rushing props is high. Linebacker injuries primarily affect rushing props and game script. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
DI04 Safety starters Deep coverage degrades with backup safeties. Affects deep passing and prevent defenses. Moderate impact on totals, lower impact on spread. Safety injuries affect deep passing props and late-game scoring. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
DI05 Interior Defensive Line (DT/NT) Run stuffing degrades with backups. Affects rushing yards up the middle. Impact is moderate on totals, higher on interior rushing props. DT injuries affect run defense efficiency. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
DI06 Multiple starters out (2+ positions) Compounding effect: each additional starter out increases the impact non-linearly. A defense missing its CB1, a pass rusher, and an ILB is not 3x worse — it may be 5-6x worse due to communication breakdowns. Multi-position injuries create the largest line movements and the highest adjustment uncertainty. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗

Offensive injury impact — by position group

How injuries at each offensive position affect expected scoring and betting lines. Note that S8 (backup-QB fade) was tested in this repository and produced a -3.39% ROI with 4/9 gates passed — the market appears to price QB injuries efficiently.

S8 (backup-QB fade) was tested and rejected by validation gates. The market may already price QB injuries efficiently.
ID Position Impact mechanism Expected line movement Source links Official source
OI01 Starting Quarterback QB is the single highest-impact position. Losing a starting QB for a backup typically moves the spread 2-4 points. The quality of the backup determines the exact shift. S8 (Backup-QB fade) tested this: -3.39% ROI, 4/9 gates. QB injuries are the primary driver of line movement. The backup's quality is the key unknown. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
OI02 Offensive Line (LT/RT/LG/RG/C) Missing a starting tackle affects pass protection and edge running. Missing the center affects snap timing and interior protection. Multiple OL out: severe impact on both passing and rushing. OL injuries affect QB props (sacks), rushing props, and total scoring. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
OI03 WR1 / Top Receiver Losing the top target affects passing volume and efficiency. Impact is moderate if depth exists (WR2/WR3). Severe if no adequate replacement. Affects passing yards props and team total. WR injuries affect passing game volume and receiver props. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
OI04 Starting Running Back RB injuries shift volume to backups. The quality gap between RB1 and RB2 is typically smaller than at other positions (running backs are more interchangeable). Moderate impact on rushing props, lower on team total. RB injuries primarily affect individual rushing props, not team totals. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
OI05 Tight End (starter) TE injuries affect red-zone targets and intermediate passing. Impact is moderate on team total, high on TE-specific props. TE injuries are position-specific and affect props more than team outcomes. NFL-STATS NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗

Lineup deficiency matrix — depth-level impact

Expected line adjustments based on the depth level of the replacement player. These estimates increase non-linearly as the replacement player drops from 2nd to 3rd to 4th string, because the talent gap widens and the player may have little or no NFL game experience.

All estimates are unverified. They are analytical expectations, not measured values. Each links to the gap or strategy that would validate it.
ID Scenario Expected line adjustment Uncertainty Verification status Official source
LD01 Defense: 2nd string replacing 1st string Expected scoring impact: +2-4 points for opposing offense. Line adjustment: 1-2 points spread, 2-4 points total. Depends on position. CB and pass rush have higher impact than safety and DT. UNVERIFIED — requires historical data on performance with/without starters NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
LD02 Defense: 3rd string replacing 1st string Expected scoring impact: +4-7 points for opposing offense. Line adjustment: 2-3 points spread, 4-6 points total. Third-string players are typically rookies or practice-squad elevations. The drop-off is severe. UNVERIFIED — no historical 3rd-string performance data available NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
LD03 Defense: 4th string replacing 1st string Expected scoring impact: +6-10 points. Line adjustment: 3-5 points spread, 5-8 points total. Fourth-string players may not have NFL game experience. Extreme uncertainty. UNVERIFIED — extremely rare; insufficient data NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
LD04 Offense: Backup QB replacing starter Measured impact (S8): -3.39% ROI on fade bets. Spread adjustment: 2-4 points. Total adjustment: 3-5 points lower. S8 tested this and found a negative ROI. The market may already price this in efficiently. Partially measured — S8 backtest exists but did not pass validation gates NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
LD05 Offense: Multiple OL starters out Expected scoring impact: -2-4 points for own offense (worse protection, worse run game). Line adjustment: 1-2 points spread against, 2-3 points total lower. OL injuries compound: 2 out is worse than 2x one out due to communication breakdowns. UNVERIFIED — no specific OL-injury study in this repository yet NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
LD06 Both sides: key injuries on both teams Net impact depends on relative severity. If both teams lose a CB1, the effect is roughly neutral on the spread but the total may go up (both secondaries weakened). Symmetric injuries affect totals more than spreads. UNVERIFIED — no multi-injury interaction model exists NFL-STATS ↗ repo ↗
