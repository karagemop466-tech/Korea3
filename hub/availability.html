Read this before using the table

Three limits are structural, not temporary. (1) The NFL defines its game-status words qualitatively — 'uncertain', 'unlikely', 'will not play' — and publishes no percentage. (2) A practice-participation line is not a game status. (3) A depth chart is a printed order, not an official designation: two of the four clubs on this page label theirs unofficial in their own words. So this page can tell you that a starter is out and who is printed next. It cannot tell you how many snaps the replacement will play, and it will not pretend to.

Every row links to the official page it came from. Anything the official pages do not state is either absent here or flagged on the Flagged for Review page.
