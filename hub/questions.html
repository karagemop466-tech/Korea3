Answered now from official sources

ID Question Answer Status Official source
QA01 May we compile NFL.com content into a dataset? No, not without express prior written consent: the Terms prohibit systematic retrieval or compilation for a collection, compilation, database or directory. answered NFL-TERMS ↗
QA02 What do the game-status labels mean? Out = will not play; Doubtful = unlikely to participate; Questionable = uncertain whether the player will play. 'Probable' was eliminated. answered NFL-INJ-POLICY ↗
QA03 What do the practice labels mean? Did Not Participate; Limited Participation = less than 100% of a player's normal repetitions; Full Participation = 100%. The practice report no longer carries 'Out'. answered NFL-INJ-POLICY ↗
QA04 How long must an injured-reserve player miss? At least four games. A club may designate up to eight players to return; a player may be designated twice and both count toward the eight. answered NFL-IR-FAQ ↗
QA05 How large are the rosters and practice squads? 53 on the active/inactive list; 48 game-day active with at least eight offensive linemen, otherwise 47; practice squad 16 (17 with an International Pathway player); a practice-squad player may be elevated for at most three regular-season games. answered NFL-ROSTER ↗
QA06 Is there an official injury report we can point to? Yes. NFL.com publishes a weekly injury report with per-club tables and the columns Player, Position, Injuries, Practice Status and Game Status. It displays no export, revision timestamp or licence statement. answered NFL-INJURIES ↗
QA07 Does the league publish betting lines? No official odds or line publication is listed in the league's own Stats & Records guidance, which points to statistics, records and the Record and Fact Book. Market prices therefore come from licensed operators, not the league. answered NFL-STATS ↗
QA08 What is the standard price and what does it pay? NFL Support states the point-spread price is most often -110; DraftKings states a -110 wager risks $110 to win $100. answered NFL-ML-ART ↗ DK-ODDS ↗
QA09 What win rate breaks even at -110? 52.381% (110 ÷ 210). This is arithmetic, not a forecast. answered DK-ODDS ↗
QA10 How many bets are needed to detect a real edge? It depends on alpha, power and the true edge. With 80% power and a one-sided 5% test, detecting 55% against a 52.381% break-even needs about 2,242 settled bets; 53% needs about 40,223. answered NIST-SAMPLESIZE ↗
QA11 How much dependence does a two-leg coin-flip parlay need? At -110 per leg, the parlay pays 3.6446; break-even joint probability is 27.438%. Against two independent 50% legs, the phi coefficient must be at least 0.0975. This is an arithmetic identity under the stated assumption. answered DK-ODDS ↗ NIST-CORR ↗
QA12 May correlated legs be parlayed? Not as an ordinary parlay. FanDuel states correlated selections are not permitted unless explicitly offered as a Same Game Parlay, and reserves the right to void a correlated parlay accepted in error. answered FD-CT-RULES ↗
