How to use this register

A decision is only reasonable when each question below is either answered or consciously accepted as open. The 'information held' column states what this repository actually has, with a link; the 'information missing' column states what it does not have. A blank answer is an unknown, rendered with an unverified marker that opens the review page — it is never filled in with a plausible value.

Rule Consequence Official source
An answered question cites an official page The answer is quotable, and the link is on the row. NFL-INJURY-POLICY ↗
An unanswered question has a blank answer It blocks the linked decision and appears on Flagged for Review. REPO ↗
A blocking question stops a verdict No strategy, rank or reward decision is made while it is open. REPO ↗
A suggestion must name its questions The suggestions register links every proposal to the questions it needs answered first. REPO ↗

Q01 Do we hold written permission or a licence to use official NFL data at scale? Every empirical value depends on lawfully held data. Without it no price, ROI or correlation can be published. NFL Terms sections 1.1 and 1.3 prohibit reproduction and systematic retrieval without prior written consent. Any licence, contract or written consent; none is on file in this repository. — unverified open — blocking NFL-TERMS ↗ REPO ↗
Q02 Is there an official, league-wide depth chart? Determines whether '1st string' can be published as an official fact or only as club-published opinion. The NFL publishes injury reports. Depth charts are club pages; New England and Detroit label theirs unofficial. — No. Club charts only, and two of four read are explicitly unofficial. answered (official) NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗ NE-DEPTH ↗ DET-DEPTH ↗
Q03 What do the injury designations mean? A status is only usable if its definition is fixed. The league's own definitions of DNP, Limited, Full, Out, Doubtful and Questionable. — Quoted verbatim on the injuries page from the league's announcement. answered (official) NFL-INJURY-POLICY ↗
Q04 Does the official injury report say whether a player is a starter or a backup? If it did, depth mapping would be unnecessary. The report's columns are Player, Position, Injuries, Practice Status, Game Status. — No. There is no depth, starter or snap field. answered (official) NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗
Q05 Is there an official snap-count source to measure replacement exposure? Without snaps, 'a backup replaced a starter' cannot be measured, only asserted. The official player-stats index lists 11 categories and none is snap counts. Any official snap feed. No snap-count category appears in the official index; the direct category URL returns the official 404. open — blocking NFL-PLAYER-STATS ↗ NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗
Q06 Is the final inactive list captured and archived? The inactive list, not the injury report, is the last word on who can play. Policy requires an explanation to the league if an omitted player is deactivated. An archived official inactive list for each game. — unverified open — blocking NFL-INJURY-POLICY ↗ REPO ↗
Q07 How is 'starter' defined for this study? The whole 2nd/3rd/4th-string question depends on a definition that survives scrutiny. Club depth charts for 4 clubs; no league designation. An agreed operational definition and its source precedence. — unverified open — blocking NE-DEPTH ↗ NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗ REPO ↗
Q08 How large a sample is needed to separate skill from luck at the effect sizes we expect? A leaderboard that ranks noise is worse than no leaderboard. NIST confidence-interval and bootstrap methods; the repository's eight gate definitions. An agreed minimum detectable effect and power target. — unverified open — blocking NIST-CI ↗ NIST-BOOT ↗ REPO ↗
Q09 Which validation gates are mandatory and what are their thresholds? Without fixed thresholds, a strategy can always be declared a pass. Eight gates are defined in the repository and applied fail-closed. Owner sign-off on the thresholds and on what counts as failure. — unverified open — blocking REPO ↗ NIST-MULTI ↗
Q10 What are the reward-pool terms? A competition with rewards needs a sponsor, an amount, a jurisdiction and payout rules before it can rank anything. The brief asks for rewards; the qualification standard exists. Sponsor, amount, eligibility, jurisdiction, payout and dispute terms. — unverified open — blocking NFL-RG ↗ REPO ↗
Q11 Which operators and markets are in scope? Prices differ by book and market; a signal is only defined against a named market. Official operator rules for spread, moneyline, total, parlay and props. A named book, market set and account jurisdiction. — unverified open — blocking DK-ODDS ↗ DK-MARKETS ↗ REPO ↗
Q12 How are pushes and ties settled in a parlay? Changes the expected value of every correlated combination. DraftKings: a push is removed and the odds are recalculated. — Answered by the operator's own rule page. answered (official) DK-PARLAY ↗
Q13 How is a player prop settled if the player does not start? Directly affects any prop-combination strategy. DraftKings: generally, if a player starts or meets the playing-time requirement, bets stand and settle accordingly. — Answered by the operator's own article; complete settlement is directed to its sport-specific house rules. answered (official) DK-PLAYER ↗
Q14 Do we hold an executable, timestamped quote archive? Without it, no ROI or closing-line value can be computed. No licensed archive is present; all price-based values are blank. An archive of executable quotes with timestamps. No. This is a standing constraint, not a data gap that can be closed from public pages. answered (official) NFL-TERMS ↗ REPO ↗
Q15 Is the availability signal strong enough to matter? Determines whether the injury and depth work becomes a strategy or stays context. One week across four clubs; no outcome data joined. A dated multi-week archive and a power analysis. — unverified open — non-blocking NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗ REPO ↗
Q16 What is the false-discovery policy for a large family of tests? Ranking many strategies without multiplicity control manufactures winners. NIST multiple-comparison and Bonferroni methods; NIH-hosted FDR literature. A chosen policy and family definition. — unverified open — blocking NIST-MULTI ↗ NIST-BONF ↗ NIH-FDR ↗
Q17 What takes precedence when two official pages disagree? Five injury-report players already fail to match a depth row; without a rule each case is ad hoc. A documented conflict list (F12) and both official pages. A precedence rule and an escalation path. — unverified open — blocking NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗ NE-DEPTH ↗ REPO ↗
Q18 Can a published snapshot be reproduced after the source changes? Every mutable official table needs a date and a live link or it becomes unverifiable. The injury page changes as practice reports are filed. An archived official copy. No archive exists; therefore every mutable row is dated and linked to the live page. answered (official) NFL-INJURY-REPORT ↗ NFL-TERMS ↗
