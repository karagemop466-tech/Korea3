Read this first

This is the integration view produced on 2026-11-02. It intentionally contains no measured NFL edge, ROI, correlation coefficient, executable price or funded reward: those values require licensed event-level official data and archived operator quotes that were not available. A blank cell means unverified, never zero, and links to the review page. Earlier exploratory backtests are preserved in the archive and are clearly separated.

Independent research. Not betting advice. Not affiliated with the NFL or any sportsbook.
