Read this first. This is an audit trail, not a picks page. The pre-registered strategy family was tested on 5,199 priced regular-season games and none of it cleared the gates. The useful output is the measurement: how large the vig is, how wide the luck band is, which leg combinations are genuinely dependent, and what would have to be true for a strategy to be worth money.

FindingWhat the data showsEvidence
No strategy earned a reward0 of 11 pre-registered strategies passed all nine validation gates. Best full-sample ROI is +0.58% (S6), and its week-block 95% interval [-7.51%, +8.17%] contains zero.evidence ↗
The winner of a small leaderboard is usually luckSimulating 11 no-skill strategies with the same bet counts produces a best strategy averaging +5.4% ROI, and a profitable 'winner' 93% of the time (95th percentile +16.3%). Any ranking of a handful of backtests must clear this band.evidence ↗
The model is close to the market on accuracy, not on calibrationSpread RMSE 13.53 points versus the market's 13.21; correlation with the result 0.389 versus 0.437. Total RMSE 13.55 versus 13.32. Being close is not enough: Brier skill against the de-vigged market is negative for every model strategy.evidence ↗
The vig is the wallAverage two-way hold in the recorded prices is 2.75%. Detecting a 3% per-bet edge at 80% power needs about 6,200 settled bets, roughly 8 seasons at 750 bets per season.evidence ↗
Same-game legs are not independent; cross-game legs areMeasured phi: moneyline vs spread 0.671, spread vs total 0.016, moneyline vs total 0.000, two different games in the same week 0.006.evidence ↗
Home and away scoring are slightly negatively correlatedr = -0.051 (95% -0.077 to -0.024, n=5,199). Combo legs built on 'both teams will score a lot' do not get a positive boost from this data.evidence ↗
Player props could not be measured, so nothing is publishedNo reachable source provides official player-level game logs or licensed prop prices. Every prop value on this site is blank and listed for review rather than estimated.evidence ↗
