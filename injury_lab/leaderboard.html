What promotion would take

  • IG3 first: 300 settled bets, 8 seasons, 40 holdout bets, 100 confirmation-window bets (docs/preregistration.md, scripts/gates.py).
  • IG5 across the whole tested family, not this family alone: the published family size is 18, and adding nine rules here means the correction applies to 27.
  • IG7 and IG8, which is where most hopeful numbers die: every bet re-priced at -115, and re-scored against the second line source in the dataset.
  • Only then IG9, the luck-adjusted edge, which is the only number on this site worth trusting with money.
  • Rewards stay unfunded and unassigned until a rule clears all of it; see the competition page in the consolidated dashboard.
