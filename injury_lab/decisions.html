S16 · Publish depth coverage as a tiered provenance matrix, and refuse the tiers a model should not read

Three club charts were captured and they are not the same kind of object: Detroit's is compiled by its football communications staff, New England's is explicitly written by the site's own writers and dissociated from the organization, and Dallas's prints no statement at all. Column counts differ too (up to 5 printed strings for Detroit, 4 for New England, mostly 2 for Dallas). A 'backup quality' feature that reads all three as one source mixes an authoritative document with a disclaimer-bearing opinion page, and a blanket 'depth available: yes' on the site would be false in a way no single row shows.

S17 · Decide the capture scale before writing another injury rule

Nine rules are pre-registered and none can be evaluated: 6 completed fixtures in the pinned dataset match a retained official capture, against a published requirement of 300 settled bets across at least 8 seasons with 40 holdout bets. At the observed fire rate the arithmetic is 22 weeks of capture for the broadest rule, 65 for the total-scoring rule and 129 for the quarterback rule that currently has the only positive pilot return. That is a decision about work, made now, rather than a slow accumulation nobody signs off on.
