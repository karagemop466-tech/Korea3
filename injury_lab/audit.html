What this changes, in the repository’s own terms

  • The parser is not the weak link. 31 of 49 overlapping rows are identical on every compared field, and position codes and player URLs differ on zero rows out of 49. A truncation defect (H04) cost no rows in the overlap, because the chunks both records reach happen to cover the same clubs.
  • The source moved. 15 designations and their injury text exist on the later day and not the earlier one, and 3 practice statuses changed. That is not a transcription dispute; it is the report being updated across the week, which is the mechanism behind H01 and the reason for S19.
  • The older file is now the weaker one, on retention alone. Its 254 rows cover far more of the page than this capture does, and none of them can be checked against a page any more (H29).
  • What this lab still cannot say. The two records do not establish why the 15 rows differ - page update or earlier bucketing choice - and the difference is not evidence about which players played. Both readings are printed in injury_lab/data/transcribe_diff.json.
