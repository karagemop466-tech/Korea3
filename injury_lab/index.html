What this directory adds

Everything here exists because the rest of the repository said it could not.

  • Injuries now reach the strategies. The root dashboard’s own limitations section says “No injury data”, and the 18-rule family was built without any. Here the official weekly report is parsed, retained and joined, so an injury rule can be computed instead of described.
  • The depth requirement is no longer recorded as impossible. Three member clubs publish an ordered chart on their own domain; 17 absence-to-replacement rows are published with both sources linked. Flag H05.
  • Captures are retained and hashed, so a transcription can be re-checked after the official page moves. It does move: flag H01.
  • The collisions that break decisions are quantified, not narrated: 15 clashing strategy ids and 8 clashing gate ids, on the leaderboard page.

What it refuses

  • No promotion and no reward. Six completed fixtures match a retained capture against a 300-bet gate, so every rule in the family reads FAIL or BLOCKED. Flag H25.
  • No imputation. A blank Game Status means the designation had not been published when the page was read; it does not mean the player was available. A fixture with no line in the pinned dataset has no line here.
  • No bookmaker page, no third-party depth-chart site, no price that is not in the pinned dataset.
  • No currency amount anywhere, so a reward figure cannot be copied off this site. Flag H28.
