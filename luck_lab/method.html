Method

How every number on this desk is produced, and what it may and may not be used for.

What a proper score is here

Brier score = mean((p − y)²), y = 1 for a won bet. Skill = 1 − BS(model)/BS(benchmark), where the benchmark is the de-vigged closing probability on the same bet. A score is proper when predicting the truth minimises it, so a strategy cannot farm the metric by hedging toward 0.5.

Why the closing line is the benchmark

It is the most information-dense price in the pinned file. Beating it on probability is a stronger claim than winning a bet, and it cannot be manufactured by staking choices — which is exactly the separation the brief asks for between signal and staking.

Clusters

game_id. A parlay's legs live inside one game and are not two observations; the iid interval is printed beside the cluster interval and `iid_inflation_cluster_over_iid` is how much the naive interval was too narrow.

Multiplicity

Benjamini-Hochberg over the 10 strategies with both probabilities present, at q < 0.1. m is published with every q so the reader can see the size of the family the p-value was adjusted for.

Interval method

Percentile bootstrap, 2,000 replicates, drawn by cluster with a documented linear congruential generator (multiplier 1664525, increment 1013904223, modulus 2^32, seed 20261119, index = state·k >> 32). Deterministic: any reviewer, in any language, reproduces the same interval. The skill ratio resamples numerator and denominator together, because the benchmark's own error is not a constant.

Coin-flip controls

Closed form, not simulated: with a fair coin on each game the expected profit is the mean of the two sides' payoffs and the variance is ((payoff_A − payoff_B)/2)². No seed and no sampling error in the estimator itself, so the baseline cannot be tuned by choosing a different RNG.

Wilson intervals

Hit-rate intervals use the Wilson score interval: centre = (p + z²/2n)/(1 + z²/n), half-width = z/(1 + z²/n)·√(p(1−p)/n + z²/4n²), z = 1.959963984540054. The formula is printed so it can be checked by arithmetic: this session has no network access, so no external statistics reference was re-opened (recorded in flagged.html).

Settlement

Side: home covers when (home − away) − spread_line > 0. Total: over when (home + away) − total_line > 0. Moneyline: the team with more points wins. Landing exactly on the number is a push and the stake is returned, so pushes keep a 0 in the ROI denominator — the convention line_lab's metric code uses. The official rulebook is linked for the tie rule; the pricing conventions are dataset-tier.

Tiers

official-league / official-club means a page a merged session opened; dataset means the pinned nflverse file; internal means a recomputation in this repository. Prices here are dataset-tier: they score, they do not cite. The brief's rule that correlations come only from official numbers is therefore not met by this desk, and the readiness register row says so.

Limits of this session

The sandbox reaches only github.com, npmjs.org and pypi.org. nfl.com is unreachable from here, so no official page was re-opened to confirm a price or a score. Everything published is arithmetic over committed files, and the reconciliation table shows where that arithmetic agrees with a surface built by a different session.
