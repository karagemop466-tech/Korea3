Tier. dataset -- closing prices from the pinned nflverse games.csv. NOT an official source. Window: seasons 1999-2026, every game with a final score and the price pair the control needs. These numbers may be used to score a strategy; they may not be cited as a league fact, and they do not satisfy the brief's requirement that correlations be computed from officially sourced numbers.

Every figure on this page is a deterministic recomputation over files committed in this repository (market prices are tier dataset, not an official league source), or a value another session recorded against an official page, labelled as such.
