Why every row says RETRO_FROZEN or BLOCKED. A rule that has already been scored on 2006-2025 cannot be pre-registered against that window: the data has been seen. The hash is real, the backtest may be published, and promotion is available only from a prospective sample recorded after the freeze. That is the discipline the brief asked for, applied honestly instead of claimed.
