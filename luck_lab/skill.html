Five equal-count bins of the model's own probability against what happened. A rule can be unbiased on average and wrong in every bin; this is where that shows up.

Benchmark. the de-vigged closing market probability on the same bet (market_prob). Brier skill = 1 - mean((p_model-y)^2)/mean((p_market-y)^2); the benchmark is the closing line, so a positive lower bound means the model beat the consensus on probability, not that one settlement went well.

Clusters. game_id. A parlay's legs live inside one game and are not two observations; the iid interval is printed beside the cluster interval and `iid_inflation_cluster_over_iid` is how much the naive interval was too narrow.

ROI convention. pnl.mean() over every staked row including pushes, matching line_lab/evaluation.py:strategy_metrics. `n_decisive` excludes pushes for the win-rate columns, which is what a break-even rate must be compared with.

Namespace. S1..S11 here are line_lab's; data/derived/gates_results.csv uses S01..S18 for other rules (flag X01).

Every figure on this page is a deterministic recomputation over files committed in this repository (market prices are tier dataset, not an official league source), or a value another session recorded against an official page, labelled as such.
