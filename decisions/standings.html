Why the official standings are on this page

Every model input in this repository comes from a dataset whose most recent completed game is 2026-10-29. The official standings read on 2026-11-12 describe a league that has played roughly a week more. Any decision taken today rests on data already behind the official record.

The staleness problem, stated precisely

The pinned dataset contains 7,075 completed regular-season games and 164 unplayed 2026 fixtures. Its last completed game is 2026-10-29. The official 2026 division standings on 2026-11-12 show clubs with eight and nine games played. The gap is about one week of games, including any result that moved a division. Nothing on the site currently says this. Proposal S12 on the suggestions page is to print the dataset date on every page and fail the build when the gap exceeds a threshold.
