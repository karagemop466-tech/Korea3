The honest position after this session

Two things are true at once, and both matter for any decision.

What holds up

Every headline number that could be recomputed from committed data was reproduced: 21 of 24 checks matched exactly, including the parlay break-even arithmetic, the correlation coefficients and the 7,075-game universe. No fabricated number was found.

What does not

Nothing in the repository has a lawful data licence, an executable price archive, a funded reward, or a power target. The best rule (strategy S14 in the gate table, cited here as G-S14) is +8.41% raw ROI over 607 bets with an FDR-adjusted q of 0.288, and 0 of 18 rules pass their gates. The leaderboard cannot rank skill because it holds no evidence of any.

The one thing that is genuinely new

The project had no injury data at all. It now publishes a dated snapshot of the official league report: 254 players across 24 clubs for 14 games. But of the 93 players who did not practise, only 5 carry a final status. The official page says who is hurt, not who will play.

How to use this section

Read the questions first, then the suggestions, then choose a path. A proposal cannot be judged solved while the question that blocks it is open.
