What counts as an official source, every page opened for this section, and the rules the verifier enforces.

What counts as an official source here

An official publisher is the organisation that publishes the fact: the NFL and its clubs and properties, licensed operators for their own rules, US standards bodies for statistical definitions, and this repository's committed files for project-state facts. A third-party dataset is never used to fill a verified value.

Verification discipline

The rules below are what the checker enforces, not aspirations.

Rules
RuleHow it is enforcedWhere
Every table row links an official publisher or the committed file it came fromdecisions/check.py fails the build otherwise.check.py
An unverifiable value is blank and links to the flag pageThe blank marker is machine-checked.flags
No network access during the buildThe build reads only committed JSON; snapshots are dated, not live.build.py
The build is deterministicRunning build.py twice yields byte-identical output; the checker re-runs and diffs.build.py
A number is never inferredWhere a quantity does not exist officially it is blank and the reason recorded.recompute.py

Reproduce

Three commands. The first needs no third-party packages; all three are standard library only.

python3 decisions/recompute.py   # re-derive every number from committed data
python3 decisions/build.py       # regenerate this section (deterministic)
python3 decisions/check.py       # fail-closed verifier for this section

Snapshots the build reads: injury snapshot, standings snapshot and recomputation output. They are dated and are not refreshed by the build.
