Two numbering schemes that look alike

A gate strategy (G-S01…G-S16) is a betting rule scored in data/derived/gates_results.csv. A proposal (S01…S15) is a change to this project, scored on this page. They share digits and nothing else. The gate table keeps its original IDs; anywhere both appear on this site a strategy is written G-S14.

How to read the scores

Each proposal is scored 1-5 on four published axes and the weighted total is computed by the build script. The weights are shown so you can disagree with them and re-rank. This is a decision aid, not a verdict.

Scoring rubric
AxisWeightWhat a 5 meansWhere the weights live
