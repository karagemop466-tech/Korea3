Executive summary

What this session found, what it adds, the five findings that matter for a decision and the next three actions — written so a reader who reads nothing else still makes a better decision.

Bottom line

The project has built a real line-origination pipeline, and it does not have an edge. That is the finding, and it is a useful one: it is stated honestly, it reproduces, and it stops the project spending money chasing a signal that is not there.

What this session adds

  • Decision readiness. 15 scored proposals and 18 questions (10 blocking) so a path can be chosen before anything is built.
  • Injury intelligence. The first injury data in the project: 254 official player rows for 2026 Week 10, with the confidence tier of every finding stated.
  • Independent recomputation. 24 checks re-derived from committed data; 21 matched exactly and no fabricated number was found.
  • Honest failure. Four of this session's own claims were wrong and are published as withdrawn on the defect register, including one caught while this page was being built.
