Paths & scoring

Three coherent paths and the full weighted ranking, with the recommended order and the reason for it. Choosing a path does not authorise data acquisition the NFL Terms prohibit.

Three paths

Pick a path rather than a pile of items: the steps inside a path depend on each other, and taking a step out of order is how a project ends up ranking luck.

Recommended order

P2 is first because it is the only path that both answers the brief's availability requirement and can be completed with official public sources. P3 is second because it is cheap and protects everything built afterwards. P1 is last because it depends on a contract the project does not control, and no amount of engineering substitutes for it.
