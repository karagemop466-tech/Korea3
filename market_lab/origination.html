Origination order

The model originates the side first, then the total, then the moneyline — in that fixed order, with each fair line published separately.

The order. The model originates its markets in a fixed order: (1) the side (spread), (2) the total, (3) the moneyline. The moneyline is originated last and is derived only from the spread produced in step 1 and its uncertainty; it never reads the market moneyline. Each fair line is published as a separate column and compared to the market line on the fair-vs-market page.
