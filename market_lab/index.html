Price snapshots, a verified results feed, the origination order, fair-vs-market columns, signal/staking separation and the demotion gate — built from pinned inputs.

What this ledger adds. The brief asked for a price-snapshot store that records opening and closing numbers for every market we originate, a verified results feed, the origination order (side, then total, then moneyline), the fair line / market line / difference published as separate columns, signal separated from staking, and a demotion gate that sends a live strategy back to paper. Each is built here from the pinned inputs; values that no pinned or official source can confirm are left blank and flagged.

Key findings

Each row links to the evidence behind it.

FindingWhat the data showsEvidence
Opening and closing cover disjoint seasonsOpening numbers exist in the pinned data only for 2021, SPREAD and TOTAL, from one sportsbook; closing numbers cover 2006-2018. No game has both, so per-game opening-to-closing pairing is not possible from this data and the missing side of every row is left blank and flagged.snapshot.html
The results feed verifies against the official score20,190 of 20,490 settled rows re-settle to the recorded outcome from the official final score; 18 mismatches and 282 missing scores are flagged, not papered over.results.html
Origination order is fixedThe model originates the side, then the total, then the moneyline; the moneyline is derived only from the originated side and never reads the market.origination.html
Fair, market and difference are separate columnsAll three markets carry the model fair line, the market line and the difference (5,199 side, 5,199 total, 10,130 moneyline rows).fair_vs_market.html
Signal and staking are ranked apart11 strategies are ranked on signal (Brier skill, luck percentile) and on staking (holdout/full ROI) independently, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal.signal_staking.html
The demotion gate is definedA live strategy returns to paper if holdout ROI, significance, luck control or market skill fails. No strategy is live today, so none is demoted — the check is published and auditable.demotion.html
