Demotion gate

The rule that sends a live strategy back to paper when its edge stops being real.

The demotion gate. A live strategy is demoted to paper if any demotion condition fails on its latest full evaluation: D1 holdout ROI > 0, D2 bootstrap CI low > 0, D3 permutation percentile >= 95, D4 Brier skill >= 0. A strategy is live only if it passed every promotion gate (G1–G9) and the demotion gate is not triggered. The check is published for every strategy, live or not, so the rule and its inputs are auditable.
