Replacement depth

Requirement 6 — how far down a club's own chart it has to reach, not merely that it has to reach.

The join, stated before the table. A row appears when the player listed at string 1 of a position in a club's own published depth chart also appears on the league's official Week 10 injury report for that club with a game status of Out, Doubtful or Questionable. The replacement is the next name the same club printed at the same position, in the club's own column order — skipping any name that is itself on the report as Out or Doubtful.

What a depth cost is and is not. It is a count of columns in a club's own publication. It is not a judgement about how good the replacement is: no ability, snap count, grade or rating is attached to it anywhere in this repository.
