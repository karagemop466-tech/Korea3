Each gate names a window, a statistic and a threshold, so a demotion is an event a reader can reproduce rather than a judgement someone made.

What the demotion gates cost

A gate that fires on a 95% upper bound will demote a genuinely break-even strategy 5% of the times it is checked. Checking more often is not free: the windows below are arithmetic, not preference.

Re-promotion

A demoted strategy does not come back by waiting. It re-qualifies by passing every promotion gate again on bets made after the demotion, including a fresh holdout window. D05 is absolute: any edit to the rule, its parameters or its market sends it back to paper immediately, because an edited rule is a new rule and a new rule has not been tested.
