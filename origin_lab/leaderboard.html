Requirement 5 — signal ranked on flat stakes, staking reported beside it and never allowed to overwrite it.

The split. Signal is scored on flat one-unit bets and nothing else. Staking is the same bets run a second time with a Kelly fraction sized from our own fair probability against the market price. Ranking on the second would let a lucky sequence of stake sizes carry a mediocre rule, which is the exact failure this requirement is written to prevent.
