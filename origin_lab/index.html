Read this before the numbers. Three facts shape everything on this site.

  • The market beats the model. Out-of-sample side error is 10.59 points against the closing line's 10.25. Same direction on the total and the moneyline. Whether that is a defect depends on what the model is for, which is question Q11, not a fact.
  • Almost no opening numbers exist. 272 of 7,239 priced games carry one, all in 2021, from one book, for two of the three markets. Closing-line value cannot be computed, so no strategy can be promoted yet.
  • No official publisher prints an NFL price. Not the league, not Football Operations, not any club. Requirement 7 says correlations come from officially sourced numbers, so four of the correlations the project wants are blank with a reason rather than computed from a third-party file.

What is here

Origination

A small walk-forward model fitted on 1999-2005 and frozen, run out-of-sample from 2006. It emits the side, then the total, then the moneyline, and the two implied team scores are pinned by the first two. Every row is checked against that identity.

Origination order · Fair / market / difference

Evidence

Every table row links the publisher it came from. A final score is a league fact; a price is not, and the site never pretends otherwise. The 2026 results feed is reconciled club by club against the league's own standings.
