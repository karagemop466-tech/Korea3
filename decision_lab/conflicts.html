The structural conclusion

Three of these discrepancies share one cause, and it is not an error by either publisher. The weekly injury report describes availability for the coming game. A player on injured reserve is not available for it, so he is not listed — which means the report cannot show the most severe availability event there is (D08). And designations are added through the week, so a Thursday capture shows almost none of them (D13).

Two consequences follow, and both are decisions rather than findings:

  • A single official feed is not enough. S02 proposes taking the union with provenance kept separate, and S03 proposes adding an official roster-status feed that the weekly report cannot substitute for.
  • Every row must carry its capture time. S05 proposes a staleness gate, because a page that looks live but is four days old is how a decision gets made on data that has already changed.
