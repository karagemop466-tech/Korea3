What this register is for.

The brief asks for a clear list of questions, information and answers that must be addressed before a decision can reasonably be made, based on what is already known and what is not, with suggestions for discovering the missing information. 18 questions are registered; 15 are marked blocking, meaning the decision named in that row cannot reasonably be made until the question is answered.

The Answer column is empty on every row. That is deliberate. check.py fails the build if an answer is present while a question is still open, so a conclusion cannot be written ahead of its evidence.
