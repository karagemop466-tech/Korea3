What this is

A dated decision pack for NFL 2026 Week 10, built to close three requirements in the project brief that the repository did not previously implement: a suggestions section with a decision gate, a register of the questions that must be answered before a decision is reasonable, and an injury and depth-availability tracker sourced only from official publishers.

How to use this pack

  1. Read Open questions first. 15 of 18 questions are marked blocking, which means the decision they name cannot reasonably be made until they are answered.
  2. Then read Suggestions. Every entry lists options with their trade-offs and is stuck at Proposed. Nothing is implemented until you choose.
  3. Use Injury and availability only as a dated snapshot, and read it alongside Source conflicts, which explains why the official report looks empty mid-week.
  4. Anything blank is blank because no official source confirms it. Every blank is flagged. No blank is an implied zero.
